What Age Should a Child Start Golf Lessons?

A start full of fun. Friendly group golf lessons for ages 6 to 16, clubs provided, every week in term time.

Junior golf lessons at Evolution Golf Academy run from age 6 to 16, and six is a genuine starting age rather than a marketing one. At that age sessions are mostly about coordination, balance and making contact with a moving target, delivered as games. Children who start at six are not being groomed for a tour card, they are learning to swing something with their hands while staying balanced, which happens to be useful for every sport.

That said, there is no right age and no window that closes. Plenty of children start at eleven or twelve after trying other sports, and teenagers who take it up at fourteen can improve very quickly because they are strong enough to hit the ball a long way and old enough to concentrate for a full hour. What matters far more than starting age is whether your child enjoys the sessions enough to come back.

Who Coaches the Junior Academy?

Will Painter, PGA Professional · TPI Certified · Trackman Expert, and Academy Director. He is the only coach at the academy, he leads every junior group himself, and he coaches adults as well, which is worth knowing if you fancy learning alongside your child. Junior coaching is a distinct skill set: holding the attention of a nine year old for an hour while still teaching a functional grip is a different job to coaching a mid handicapper.

Because Will takes the sessions himself, the coach who saw your child in September is the coach who sees them in March, and nothing has to be re-explained. He works to PGA standards on safeguarding and conduct, and you can read his full background on the meet the team page. Behind the coaching sit TPI certified principles and Trackman expertise, used sparingly with the younger ones and in full with the older ones.

How Are Junior Golf Lessons Structured?

As friendly group golf lessons, every week in term time. That is the whole shape of the junior academy and it is deliberate. A weekly slot at the same time, with the same group and the same coach, is what turns golf into a habit for a child rather than a thing they did twice last spring. Term time keeps it lined up with the school year, so nobody is trying to get a nine year old to a golf lesson on the second Tuesday of the summer holidays.

The group format does most of the heavy lifting. Golf can be a solitary sport, and a group gives it the social element that keeps children turning up, plus a bit of friendly competition that drives effort more effectively than any coach can. Groups are also considerably cheaper than individual coaching, which matters when you are not yet sure whether the interest will last. Children are grouped by age and stage rather than lumped in together, and clubs are provided throughout.

If a keen junior wants extra work on something specific ahead of a competition, a 1:1 golf lesson can be booked separately alongside the weekly group. That is an addition to the junior academy rather than a replacement for it. Ring 07710 582036 to find out which group has space and what time it runs.

What Happens in a Junior Golf Session?

An hour that a child will want to repeat. Skills are taught, but they are taught through activity rather than instruction.

Games, Not Lectures

Targets, scoring, challenges and competition between the group. Children learn a great deal while thinking they are just playing, which is exactly the intention.

Fundamentals That Last

Grip, posture and balance taught early and revisited constantly. A child who learns these at eight never has to unlearn them at eighteen, which saves years.

Short Game and Putting

Children love the short game because success is immediate and the ball goes in the hole. It also happens to be where scoring comes from, which is a happy coincidence.

Trackman, Used Sparingly

Older juniors enjoy seeing ball speed and carry numbers, and it makes practice competitive. Younger ones do not need it and mostly do not get it.

● What parents ask first

Does My Child Need Their Own Golf Clubs?

Not to start. Clubs are provided for junior sessions, which spares you buying a set your child grows out of in fourteen months. Junior clubs are sized by height rather than age, and a child swinging cut-down adult clubs learns a compensated swing because the shafts are too heavy and too stiff for them.

When the time comes, ask us before you buy. A basic junior set is inexpensive and second-hand ones hold up well, since most are barely used. Once a teenager is playing regularly and has stopped growing quickly, custom fitting on Trackman is worth doing. Fittings here are brand neutral, so you can buy from us or anywhere you like.

Five steps from first enquiry to a child who asks to go back, which is the only measure that counts.

Ring 07710 582036 with their age, whether they have played and how they are with group activities. Will suggests a suitable group rather than putting them wherever there is a space.

Once a child can make contact reliably, playing a few holes at Ormonde Fields is the natural next step, along with junior competitions for those who want them and no pressure on those who do not.

● For parents

What Do Parents Need to Know?

Is Golf Safe for Children?

Yes, with the obvious caveat that a golf club swung near another person is dangerous, which is why spacing and awareness are taught in the first session and enforced in every one after. Juniors work in separated bays with clear distances between them, coaching is PGA qualified and DBS checked, and the covered range keeps everyone in one supervised area. Golf has a far lower injury rate than most team sports children play.

How Do You Keep Children Interested?

By making the session about doing rather than listening. Long explanations lose a nine year old inside ninety seconds, so the coaching is delivered through games with scores, targets and challenges attached. Children who are enjoying themselves absorb technique without noticing, and children who are bored absorb nothing regardless of how good the instruction is. That is the whole philosophy, and it is not complicated.

Are There Junior Golf Competitions?

There are, for those who want them. Some children thrive on competition and want a card in their hand from the start, others would much rather hit balls with their friends and never keep score. Both are entirely valid ways to enjoy golf, and neither is pushed on a child who is not asking for it. Will tells you honestly where your child sits, which is not always where a parent expects.

Can Parents Learn Too?

A good number do, and it makes practice between sessions far easier to arrange. Adults can book 1:1 golf lessons, join the monthly programme, or start from nothing with beginner coaching. Mothers who would rather learn among other women can look at the ladies academy, and Get Into Golf is the relaxed group option for anybody starting cold.

Families travel to the junior academy from Derby, Nottingham, Ripley, Codnor, Heanor and across Derbyshire and Nottinghamshire. The site is fifteen minutes from Derby and twenty from Nottingham on the A610, parking is free, and there is somewhere warm to sit while you wait. Directions are on the location page.
