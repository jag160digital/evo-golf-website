What Do Junior Golf Lessons Include?

Junior golf tuition at Evolution Golf Academy is age-appropriate and led by a PGA Professional at every session - not a group activity leader. Sessions can be arranged around school hours, seven days a week.

Swing Foundations

Grip, stance, swing path and ball striking built correctly from day one. Good technique at 8 years old is still good technique at 18 - and bad habits formed early take years to undo.

Book a session →

Course Play and Etiquette

Rules, etiquette, scoring and how to think around a golf course. Junior golfers learn to play golf - not just hit balls on a driving range.

Short game area →

Trackman Data Feedback

The same launch monitor technology used with adults. Juniors quickly understand the link between what they do and what the data shows - which makes progress faster.

Junior Club Fitting

Custom fitting for junior golfers through Hayden Berridge PGA - club length, shaft flex and lie angle matched to your child's swing and growth stage, not a scaled-down adult spec.

Who Coaches the Junior Academy?

Scott Hassall is a PGA Professional and Head of Juniors at Evolution Golf Academy. His sessions balance genuine technical development with coaching that keeps young players engaged and coming back. Scott adjusts his approach for each age group rather than running the same session for everyone - a 6-year-old's lesson looks quite different to a 15-year-old's.
