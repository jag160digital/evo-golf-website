The right clubs make a bigger difference than most people realise

Most golfers buy clubs based on what looks good, what a friend plays, or what's on sale. The shaft flex, loft, lie angle, length and grip size are almost always wrong for their specific swing. Then they spend years taking lessons to fix a ball flight problem that was caused by the equipment in the first place.

A Trackman fitting with Hayden Berridge removes the guesswork. You hit real balls with real clubs and the data shows what's happening with each combination. At the end of the session, you know exactly what you need - and why.

What your fitting session covers

Shaft analysis

Flex, weight, kick point and profile - tested against your actual swing speed, tempo and transition. The shaft is the engine. Get this wrong and nothing else matters.

Head specification

Loft, lie angle, offset, face angle. Trackman shows how each combination affects ball flight and ball speed so the decision is based on data, not guesswork.

Driver & woods

Optimising your long game - finding the setup that gives you the best combination of distance and accuracy for your swing speed and angle of attack.

Irons & wedges

Gapping analysis to make sure you have consistent yardage gaps throughout the bag. Missing a club in your bag is costing you shots you don't even notice losing.

Length & grip

Club length and grip size affect your impact position directly. Most golfers are playing with clubs that are the wrong length for their height and build.

Full report

You leave with a complete specification document - what to buy, what specs to ask for, and what to tell any pro shop or manufacturer. No ambiguity.

Club fitting FAQs

Do I need to be a low handicapper to benefit from a fitting?+
Not at all. In fact, beginners and high handicappers often benefit the most. Playing with the wrong equipment - usually standard off-the-shelf specs - makes learning harder than it needs to be.
How long does a fitting session take?+
A full bag fitting typically takes 90 minutes to 2 hours. Individual club fittings (driver only, irons only, wedges) can be done in 60 minutes. Get in touch and we'll confirm based on what you need.
Do I need to buy clubs from you after the fitting?+
No. The fitting gives you a specification document that you can take to any retailer or manufacturer. We want you to have the right clubs - where you buy them is up to you.
What if I've just bought new clubs - should I still get fitted?+
Yes, because the fitting will tell you whether those clubs are actually right for you. If they're not, Hayden can advise on adjustments (loft/lie bending is often straightforward) or flag what to look for when you next buy.
