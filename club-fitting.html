What Is Custom Golf Club Fitting?

Custom golf club fitting near Derby and Nottingham at Evolution Golf Academy uses Trackman technology to match your clubs to your actual swing. Hayden Berridge PGA, specialist fitter and PGA professional, carries out all fitting sessions at our facility in Codnor, Ripley - 15 minutes from Derby and 20 minutes from Nottingham on the A610. Custom fitting covers shaft flex, loft, lie angle, club length and grip size - all tested against data from your real swing, not an estimate of what an average golfer needs.

Most golfers buy clubs based on what looks good, what a friend plays, or what's on sale. The shaft flex, loft and lie angle are almost always wrong for their specific swing. Then they spend years in golf lessons trying to fix a ball flight problem that was caused by the equipment all along. A Trackman fitting with Hayden Berridge removes that guesswork - you hit real balls with real clubs and the data shows what's happening with each combination.

What Does a Trackman Club Fitting Include?

Shaft analysis

The shaft is fundamental to your golf swing. Flex, weight, kick point and profile are tested against your actual swing speed, tempo and transition - not a generic recommendation based on your handicap.

Head specification

Loft, lie angle, offset, face angle. Trackman shows exactly how each combination affects ball flight and ball speed so the decision is based on data, not guesswork.

Driver and woods

Finding the setup that gives you the best combination of distance and accuracy for your swing speed and angle of attack - tested with multiple head and shaft combinations.

Irons and wedges

Gapping analysis to ensure consistent yardage gaps throughout your bag. Missing a club in your bag costs shots you never even notice losing until the data shows it.

Length and grip

Club length and grip size affect your impact position directly. Most golfers are playing with clubs that are the wrong length for their height and build.

Full specification report

You leave with a complete document - what to buy, what specs to ask for, and what to tell any retailer or manufacturer. No ambiguity, no second-guessing.

Do I Need a Golf Club Fitting? - Signs You Do

Wrong equipment is one of the most common - and most overlooked - reasons golfers in Derbyshire and Nottinghamshire stop improving. If any of the following sounds familiar, a custom golf club fitting near Derby or Nottingham is worth booking.

  • ✓ Your ball flight is inconsistent and you can't work out why, even after golf lessons
  • ✓ You bought clubs off the shelf or inherited them without a fitting
  • ✓ Your clubs feel uncomfortable or you struggle to control direction
  • ✓ You've changed your golf swing significantly and haven't refitted since
  • ✓ You're significantly above or below average height for your build
  • ✓ You're a junior golfer playing with scaled-down adult clubs
  • ✓ You've hit a scoring plateau and lessons alone haven't shifted it
  • ✓ You've never had a fitting and don't know whether your clubs suit you

Custom Golf Club Fitting Derby & Nottingham - FAQs

Do I need to be a low handicap golfer to benefit from a custom fitting?+
Not at all. Beginners and high handicappers often benefit most. Playing with off-the-shelf clubs - almost never the right specification - makes learning harder than it needs to be. A fitting at any stage gives you the right tools for your swing right now.
How long does a custom golf club fitting take?+
A full bag fitting typically takes 90 minutes to two hours. Single-club fittings (driver only, irons only, wedges) can be done in around 60 minutes. Get in touch and we'll confirm based on what you need.
Do I have to buy clubs from EGA after a custom fitting?+
No. The fitting gives you a specification document that you can take to any retailer or manufacturer. We want you to have the right clubs - where you buy them is up to you.
I've just bought new clubs - should I still get a fitting?+
Yes. The fitting will confirm whether those clubs are actually right for you. If they're not, Hayden can advise on adjustments - loft and lie bending is often straightforward - or flag what to look for when you next buy.
