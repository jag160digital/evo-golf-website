What Do Beginner Golf Lessons Include?

Every beginner golf coaching session at Evolution Golf Academy is built from the foundations up, using Trackman data to show you exactly what is happening with your golf swing - so you measure your improvement, not just feel it.

Grip, Stance & Alignment

The things most beginners get wrong because nobody explained them. We fix these foundations first - your golf swing will never be better than the platform it starts from.

What to Expect in Your First Session.

Most people put off their first golf lesson because they are not sure what to expect. At Evolution Golf Academy, beginner golf lessons start with you - where you are now, what you want from your golf, and the most direct route to getting there. Will Painter, our Head Coach and PGA Professional, has taught hundreds of complete beginners across Derbyshire and Nottinghamshire.

  • ✓No jargon, no judgement - coaching starts exactly where you are
  • ✓Hitting balls from minute one - not standing in a field being talked at
  • ✓Trackman on every shot - the difference is visible, not just felt
  • ✓A simple practice plan - you leave knowing exactly what to work on
