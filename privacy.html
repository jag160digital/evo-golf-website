● Who we are

Who Controls Your Data?

This page is a working draft. It should be reviewed against your actual data handling before you rely on it.

Evolution Golf Academy, Ormonde Fields Golf Club, Nottingham Road, Codnor, Ripley, Derbyshire DE5 9RJ, is the data controller for information collected through this website. You can reach us on 07710 582036 or through the contact page.

What Information Do We Collect?

Only what you give us. If you complete an enquiry form we collect your name, email address, phone number if you provide one, the service you are interested in and anything you write in the message field. If you request the practice guide we collect your first name and email address.

Bookings made through our booking system are handled by that provider under their own privacy policy. Payments are not processed by this website.

Why Do We Collect It?

To answer your enquiry, arrange your lesson or session, and keep a record of what was agreed. If you opted in to coaching tips when requesting the practice guide, we use your email address for that until you unsubscribe.

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing.

● Your rights

What Rights Do You Have Over Your Data?

Under UK data protection law you can ask us to show you what we hold about you, correct anything wrong, delete it, restrict how we use it, or send it to you in a portable format. You can also object to us using it for marketing at any time.

To exercise any of those, call 07710 582036 or write to us at the address above. We will respond within one month. If you are not satisfied with how we handle it, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

How Long Do We Keep It?

Enquiries and coaching records are kept for as long as you are a customer and for a reasonable period afterwards, so that a coach picking up your file knows what you have already worked on. Marketing contacts are kept until you unsubscribe.

Cookies and Analytics

This site uses cookies where necessary for it to function. Embedded content such as the Google Map on our location page and the external booking system may set their own cookies under their own policies. You can block cookies in your browser settings, though parts of the site may not work as expected if you do.
