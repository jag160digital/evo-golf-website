What Is a Trackman Driving Range?

Most driving ranges near Derby and Nottingham give you a mat, a net, and no information at all. You hit balls, they land, you hit more. Whether you are improving or entrenching bad habits in your golf swing is anybody's guess.

Our Trackman driving range measures everything. Ball speed, club head speed, spin rate, launch angle, carry distance and shot shape - displayed in real time on screen in front of you. You know immediately what worked and what did not, and why it happened.

It is the same Trackman technology used on the PGA Tour and by every PGA Professional at this academy. Available to walk-in visitors from £4 a basket, no booking required.

What Does Trackman Measure at Our Derby & Nottingham Range?

Data that gives you a genuine understanding of your golf swing - not just numbers that look impressive on a screen.

Ball speed and carry distance

See exactly how far you are hitting each club and at what ball speed. Identify which clubs are giving you the most carry distance for the swing speed you have.

Launch angle and spin rate

Too much spin kills distance. Too little and you lose control. Trackman shows you both - and your PGA golf coach can use this data to optimise your ball flight and club selection.

Shot shape and direction

Club path, face angle and where the ball finishes. If you are fighting a consistent fade or draw on the driving range, the Trackman data shows exactly what is causing it.

Trackman Driving Range Derby & Nottingham - FAQs

Do I need to book the Trackman range in advance?+
No booking needed for standard Trackman driving range sessions. All 7 bays are walk-in. Private bays for coaching sessions or club fitting should be booked in advance by calling 07710 582036.
What is the difference between a Trackman range and a normal driving range?+
Most driving ranges near Derby give you a mat and a net. Our Trackman range gives you live data on every single shot - ball speed, spin, launch angle, carry distance, shot shape. You know what is happening with every swing, which means your practice actually means something for your golf game.
Can I book a golf coaching session at the Trackman range?+
Yes, and we would encourage it. Your PGA coach books a private Trackman bay, coaches you through the session, and the data from your swing drives the lesson directly. It is how all our coaching works.
Are the driving range bays covered?+
Yes. All 7 Trackman bays are fully covered so you can practise regardless of the weather. We also have a grass driving range for when conditions are good.
What are the opening hours for the driving range?+
We are open 7am to 8pm every day of the week. Call 07710 582036 if you need to check availability for a specific time slot.
