Not just a driving range - a data-driven practice facility

Most driving ranges tell you nothing useful. You hit balls, they land, you hit more. Whether you're improving or entrenching bad habits is anyone's guess.

Our Trackman bays measure every shot: ball speed, club head speed, spin rate, launch angle, carry distance, and shot shape. In real time, on screen in front of you. You know immediately what worked and what didn't - and why.

It's the same technology the world's best players use on tour. Available to walk-in visitors from £4 a basket.

What Trackman measures on every shot

Data that actually helps you understand your game - not just numbers on a screen.

Ball speed & carry

See exactly how far you're hitting it and at what ball speed. Identify which clubs are giving you the best distance for the swing speed you have.

Launch angle & spin

Too much spin is killing your distance. Too little and you lose control. Trackman shows you both - and your coach can use this to optimise your ball flight.

Shot shape & direction

Club path, face angle, and where the ball finishes. If you're fighting a consistent fade or draw, the data shows exactly what's causing it.

Trackman range - FAQs

Do I need to book a range bay in advance?+
No booking needed for standard range sessions. All 7 Trackman bays are walk-in. Private bays for lessons or fitting sessions should be booked in advance.
What's the difference between your range and a normal driving range?+
Most ranges give you a mat and a net. Ours gives you Trackman data on every single shot - ball speed, spin, launch angle, carry, shot shape. You know what's happening with every swing, which means your practice actually means something.
Can I pair a range session with a lesson?+
Yes, and we'd encourage it. Your coach can book you a private Trackman bay, coach you through the session, and the data from your swing informs the lesson directly. It's how our coaching works.
Are the bays covered?+
Yes. All 7 bays are fully covered so you can practice regardless of the weather. We also have a grass driving range for when conditions are good.
What are the opening hours?+
We're open 7am to 8pm every day of the week. Call 07710 582036 if you need to check availability for a specific time.
