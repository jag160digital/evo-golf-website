What Is a Trackman Driving Range?

Most driving ranges give you a mat, a net, and no feedback. You hit balls, they land, you hit more. Whether you're improving or entrenching bad habits is anybody's guess. Our Trackman range measures everything - ball speed, club head speed, spin rate, launch angle, carry distance and shot shape. Available to walk-in visitors from £4 a basket, no booking required.

Ball Speed & Distance

See exactly how far you are hitting each club and at what ball speed. Identify which clubs are giving you the most carry distance for your swing speed.

Launch Angle & Spin

Too much spin kills distance. Too little and you lose control. Trackman shows you both, so your PGA coach can optimise your ball flight and club selection.

Shot Shape & Direction

Club path, face angle and where the ball finishes. If you are fighting a consistent fade or draw, the Trackman data shows exactly what is causing it.
