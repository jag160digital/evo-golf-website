Where Can You Get Golf Lessons in Heanor?

Heanor golfers are close enough that Evolution Golf Academy functions as a local range rather than a destination. It is about 8 minutes from Heanor, down through Loscoe and Langley Mill to Codnor.

That short hop gets you a six bay Trackman range, an indoor simulator, a private teaching bay, a grass range and a golf fitness suite on a single site, with free parking and no membership to join.

For anyone who has been making do with hitting balls into a net or driving considerably further for coaching that actually measures something, it is a straightforward upgrade.

What Can Heanor Golfers Use the Academy For?

Most Heanor golfers who come to us use it in two ways: regular short range sessions with Trackman running to keep an eye on their numbers, and periodic lessons with a PGA Professional when something goes wrong they cannot diagnose themselves. Being eight minutes away makes both realistic on a weeknight.

How Do You Get Here From Heanor?

About 8 minutes by car via the A6007 and A610, with free parking directly outside the range.

1

From Heanor town centre

Head north on the A6007 through Loscoe towards Langley Mill, then join the A610 west to Codnor. Around 8 minutes.

2

From Langley Mill and Aldercar

Straight up the A610 towards Ripley and the academy is on Nottingham Road at Codnor.

3

From Marlpool and Shipley

Cut across to Heanor and follow the same route north, or come round via Codnor Park.

4

From Smalley and Kilburn

North on the A608 to Heanor then across to Codnor, or the country roads via Loscoe if you know them.

5

Parking and arrival

Free parking directly outside the driving range and teaching studio, so you park and walk straight to your bay. If it is your first lesson, arrive ten minutes early so your coach can find out what you want from it before the clock starts.
