## The two-step assessment (About page)
**Step 1 · PGA Professional Golf Swing Assessment** - uses Trackman, video
analysis, 3D data and coaching experience to identify:
- your current ball flight tendencies
- the swing characteristics limiting your performance
- your biggest opportunities for improvement
- whether your current swing matches your goals
- whether the limitations are technical, physical or both

**Step 2 · TPI Movement Screen** - identifies:
- mobility or stability restrictions affecting your swing
- movement patterns limiting your performance
- physical limitations preventing swing changes
- areas that could increase power, consistency and efficiency
- whether further support from a fitness professional or physiotherapist would help
