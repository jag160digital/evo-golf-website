● The turning point

Our story.

Evolution Golf Academy was founded in 2023 by Will Painter, the academy's Academy Director and an Advanced PGA Professional. Will has represented England Golf, has multiple professional wins to his name and spent 15 years playing at an elite level, which is a career most golfers would take without hesitation. It is also a career that stalled, and the reason it stalled is why this academy exists at all.

The answer wasn't the swing, it was the body.

Will spent years chasing his own game the way most committed golfers do, by working harder on technique. More lessons, more range balls, more positions. The improvement never arrived, and the honest conclusion when he finally went looking for it was uncomfortable: his body simply wasn't capable of consistently producing the movements he was trying to make. No amount of coaching was going to change that, because coaching was never the missing piece.

Fixing mobility, stability and movement quality alongside the coaching is what turned it around, and it is what the whole academy is built on. That is why every golfer here can be looked at twice, once as a golf swing and once as a body, and why TPI screening and the golf fitness suite sit next to the teaching bay rather than in a different building or a different postcode.

Why Does That Matter to an Ordinary Club Golfer?

Because most golfers are handed a technical answer to a physical problem, and then blame themselves when it does not stick. If your lead hip will not rotate, drilling a hip turn produces a compensation rather than a swing. Being told which of those you are dealing with, in an hour rather than over three seasons, is the single most useful thing coaching can do for you. It is the first question asked in every 1:1 golf lesson here.

What Has the Academy Become Since 2023?

A site with seven Trackman range bays, two or more indoor simulators, a private teaching bay, a grass driving range, a short game area and a fitness suite, open seven days a week from 7am to 8pm and open to the public. Membership exists and brings priority booking and better rates, but nothing on the site is withheld from anyone who has not joined.

● Why we are here

Why Was Evolution Golf Academy Set Up in Ripley, Derbyshire?

Evolution Golf Academy exists because of a gap that anyone who has tried to improve at golf in the East Midlands will recognise straight away. Elite coaching and serious practice technology were available, but almost always behind a private club membership, a joining fee and a committee, or else at the end of a forty five minute drive to somewhere that could fit you in three weeks on Tuesday afternoon. Golfers were being asked to choose between convenience and quality, and quality usually lost.

So we built a golf academy in Ripley, Derbyshire where the coaching is Advanced PGA Professional standard, the technology is Trackman, and neither of those depends on you joining anything. The academy sits at Ormonde Fields Golf Club on Nottingham Road in Codnor, deliberately placed on the A610 between the two cities, so that golfers coming from Derby and golfers coming from Nottingham arrive in roughly the same time. Fifteen minutes one way, twenty the other, free parking at the end of both.

What Does Trackman Official Performance Centre Mean?

Trackman is the dual-radar launch monitor used on the PGA Tour and by most of the world's leading coaches, and Official Performance Centre status means the technology here is the full specification rather than a cut-down version, and that the coach using it is trained on it. In practice it means seven range bays reporting live data, a teaching bay set up for video and Trackman together, and custom fitting decided by numbers instead of a chart on the wall. Those fittings are brand neutral, so you can buy from us or anywhere you like.

What Does TPI Certified Mean for an Ordinary Golfer?

The Titleist Performance Institute framework connects the golf swing to the body producing it, and coaching here works to TPI certified principles. A TPI certified coach can tell whether you cannot turn your hips because nobody has taught you to, or because your hips physically will not do it. That distinction saves people years. It runs through our TPI screening at £75 and through the work in the golf fitness suite, and it quietly shapes every lesson taught here.

How Big Is the Academy?

Larger than most people expect from the road, and all of it is open to the public, member or not. Seven covered floodlit bays on the Trackman driving range, two or more indoor simulators, a private teaching bay, a grass driving range for the drier months, a full short game area and a fitness suite. All of it on one site, all of it open from seven in the morning until eight at night, every day of the week.

● How we assess a golfer

What Happens in the Two-Step Assessment?

Before anyone is given a swing change, we look at the swing and then at the body producing it. Two steps, in that order, and the second one is the step almost nobody else takes.

Step 1 · PGA Professional Golf Swing Assessment

Trackman, video analysis, 3D data and coaching experience, used together in the Trackman teaching bay to work out what your golf swing is actually doing rather than what it feels like it is doing. It identifies:

  • your current ball flight tendencies
  • the swing characteristics limiting your performance
  • your biggest opportunities for improvement
  • whether your current swing matches your goals
  • whether the limitations are technical, physical or both

Step 2 · TPI Movement Screen

A Titleist Performance Institute movement screen, carried out by a TPI certified coach, which takes about an hour and involves no golf clubs at all. TPI screening is £75 on its own and is included in the monthly programme. It identifies:

  • mobility or stability restrictions affecting your swing
  • movement patterns limiting your performance
  • physical limitations preventing swing changes
  • areas that could increase power, consistency and efficiency
  • whether further support from a fitness professional or physiotherapist would help

Put the two together and you get a plan that is aimed at the thing actually holding you back. Sometimes that is a grip. Sometimes it is a hip that will not turn. Guessing between those two is how golfers lose years, and it is why the screen sits at the front of the process rather than being sold as an add-on later.

● The facilities

What Facilities Does the Golf Academy Have On Site?

Everything below is on one site in Codnor, which means a session can move from a lesson to a practice bay to the short game area without anybody getting in a car. That sounds like a small thing and it is not. It is the reason a swing change here tends to survive contact with an actual golf course.

Practice and Play

  • Trackman driving range - seven covered floodlit bays, walk-in, baskets from £4
  • Trackman simulator - indoor course play and practice at £25 an hour
  • Grass driving range - real turf and honest strike feedback through the drier months
  • Short game area - chipping, pitching, bunker play and putting
  • Private bay hire at £15 an hour when you want to work undisturbed

Coaching and Assessment

Lessons run in the private Trackman teaching bay, which pairs the launch monitor with video analysis and training aids in a space where nobody is watching you. A standard 1:1 golf lesson is £55, and the monthly programme at £60 a month is the option most regular golfers settle on once they have had a few.

For golfers who want to know what their body is contributing, TPI screening costs £75 and produces a physical picture that the coaching plan is then built around. Follow-up work happens in the golf fitness suite, which is set up specifically for the demands of the golf swing rather than general gym work.

Group Coaching and Beginners

Group sessions carry a lot of the week. Get Into Golf is for people who have never played, beginner coaching picks things up from there, the junior academy handles ages six to sixteen and the ladies academy runs women-only groups. We can lend you a set for any of them, so a first session costs you nothing in equipment.

Custom Club Fitting

Fitting is done on Trackman by our fitters, working alongside the coaching, so the specification accounts for where your swing is going rather than only where it sits today. Fittings are brand neutral, so you can buy from us or anywhere you like. Shaft, flex, lie angle, loft and swing weight are tested rather than assumed, and you leave with the numbers behind the recommendation.
