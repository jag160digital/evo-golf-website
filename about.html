About the Academy

Golf Academy in Codnor, Ripley - Between Derby & Nottingham

Evolution Golf Academy is the modern indoor and outdoor golf facility for Derby, Nottingham and the wider East Midlands. Three PGA Professionals. One fully-equipped facility. All golfers welcome.

What the academy is built on

Evolution Golf Academy exists because the standard of coaching and facility available to most golfers in this part of the East Midlands didn't match what the game deserves. You shouldn't need a private club membership or a 45-minute drive to access Trackman technology and a PGA coach who actually knows what they're doing.

The academy was built around the idea that elite standards of coaching and technology shouldn't only be available to elite players. Every golfer who books a session at EGA gets the same quality of instruction and the same Trackman data as someone who plays off scratch - because that's what's needed to actually improve.

We're a Trackman Official Performance Centre, TPI certified, and all three coaches are PGA Professionals. The facility includes covered Trackman range bays, indoor simulators, a custom fitting suite, a grass range and a short game area.

Meet your coaches

Three PGA Professionals with individual specialisms - you get the right coach for what you're working on.

🏌️

Will Painter

Head Coach & Academy Director - PGA Professional

Will leads coaching at Evolution Golf Academy, specialising in technique, swing mechanics and player development from complete beginner through to advanced golfers. He's the first coach most new members meet, and the one most return to.

🔧

Hayden Berridge

Coach & Custom Fitter - PGA Professional

Hayden runs all custom fitting sessions at the academy using Trackman data to precisely match equipment to each player's swing. He also coaches - bringing a technical understanding of how equipment specification and swing mechanics interact.

⛳

Scott Hassall

Coach & Head of Juniors - PGA Professional

Scott leads the junior academy for players aged 6-16, with structured programmes that develop technique, course management and a genuine enthusiasm for the game. He also coaches adult players across all levels.
