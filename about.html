Who We Are

Evolution Golf Academy was founded to bring world-class golf coaching and technology to the East Midlands. Located at Ormonde Fields Golf Club in Ripley, we combine expert PGA Professional coaching with Trackman Official Performance Centre facilities and a full range of coaching programmes for all ages and abilities.

Our Mission

To deliver effective, data-driven golf coaching that helps golfers improve quickly and sustainably. Whether you are beginning your golf journey or working towards a low handicap, we provide the coaching, technology and support to help you achieve your goals.

Why Choose Evolution Golf Academy?

We are committed to delivering results through a combination of expert coaching, modern technology and a supportive environment.

PGA Professionals

3 fully qualified PGA Professionals with over 50 years combined experience. Every coach uses Trackman technology to deliver data-driven lessons.

Trackman Official Centre

Authorised Trackman Official Performance Centre with the latest in golf technology. Accurate swing data on every shot, from range to course.

Complete Facilities

Trackman range, indoor simulator, teaching bay, grass range, short game area, golf fitness suite and recovery facilities all in one location.

About Evolution Golf Academy in Ripley, Derbyshire

Evolution Golf Academy was founded to bring PGA-standard golf coaching and Trackman technology to golfers in Derbyshire and the East Midlands without requiring a private club membership or a long drive. Golf lessons in Ripley from three PGA Professionals, with the same standard of instruction and Trackman data whether you're a complete beginner or an established club golfer.

The academy is built around one principle: every golfer who books a golf lesson at EGA gets the same quality of instruction and the same Trackman data, regardless of handicap. What improves a scratch player's swing is exactly what improves a beginner's - the physics doesn't change.

Evolution Golf Academy is a Trackman Official Performance Centre, TPI certified, and all three coaches hold PGA Professional status. The facility includes covered Trackman range bays, indoor simulator bays, a custom fitting suite, a grass driving range and a short game practice area in Ripley, Derbyshire.

PGA Golf Coaches at Evolution Golf Academy, Ripley, Derbyshire

Three PGA Professional golf coaches with individual specialisms. Whether you need beginner golf tuition, swing improvement coaching or custom club fitting, Evolution Golf Academy in Ripley has the right golf instructor for your game.

Will Painter

Head Coach & Academy Director - PGA Professional

Will leads all coaching at Evolution Golf Academy in Ripley, Derbyshire, specialising in golf swing mechanics, technique and player development from complete beginner through to advanced club golfers. As Head Coach, he works across all ability levels and is the first golf instructor most new students meet.

🔧

Hayden Berridge

Coach & Custom Fitter - PGA Professional

Hayden runs custom club fitting sessions at the Ripley academy using Trackman data to match equipment to each individual's golf swing. As a PGA Professional and certified fitter, he brings a technical understanding of how equipment specification and swing mechanics interact for golfers from across Derbyshire.

Scott Hassall

Coach & Head of Juniors - PGA Professional

Scott leads the junior golf academy at EGA in Ripley for players aged 6-16, with structured programmes that develop technique, course management and a love of the game. He also coaches adult golfers from Derby, Nottingham and across Derbyshire at all levels.
