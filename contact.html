How Do You Book a Golf Lesson at Evolution Golf Academy?

Fill in the form and it lands with the coaching team directly. Tell us what you play off, what you would like to change and roughly when you can get here, and we will come back with a recommendation and a time. We answer within a working day. If you would rather talk it through, ring 07710 582036 any day between 7am and 8pm.

Where to find the golf academy

Call 07710 582036. The academy is open from 7am to 8pm, seven days a week, including weekends and bank holidays. The covered range bays are floodlit, so late sessions run the same in December as they do in June.

What happens after you enquire

A coach reads your enquiry rather than an inbox robot. We reply with a suggested session, a couple of times that fit what you told us, and an honest view on whether the thing you asked for is the thing you need. We answer within a working day.

● Choosing what to book

Which Golf Lesson or Service Should You Book First?

Most people ringing to book golf lessons near Derby and Nottingham for the first time are not sure what to ask for, which is entirely reasonable given that the price list runs from a £4 basket of balls to a full custom fitting. Below is the short version of what each thing is for. If none of it quite matches your situation, send the enquiry anyway and describe the problem in your own words. Working out what you need is part of the job.

You Have Never Played Golf Before

Start with Get Into Golf. It assumes nothing, we can lend you a set for the lesson, and it is deliberately built for people who feel self-conscious about being a beginner in front of golfers. From there, beginner coaching takes you to a swing that repeats and a set of shots you can take onto a course without dreading the first tee.

You Play Already and Want to Fix Something Specific

Book a 1:1 golf lesson at £55 for sixty minutes. An hour in the private teaching bay with Trackman and video is normally enough to identify what is going on, and you will leave with one change and a practice task rather than a list. If it turns out to need more than one session, the coach will say so plainly.

You Want to Improve Steadily Over a Season

The monthly coaching programme at £60 a month is the option most regular golfers end up on. It is continuous coaching with a rolling practice plan, and progress is tracked on Trackman so you can see the direction of travel rather than relying on how last Sunday happened to go.

You Just Want to Hit Balls

Turn up. The Trackman driving range is walk-in with no booking, open from 7am to 8pm with baskets from £4 and club hire included at no extra cost. A private bay is £15 an hour if you would rather work undisturbed, and the grass driving range runs through the drier months for anyone who wants real turf under the ball.

The Weather Is Awful or You Are Booking for a Group

The Trackman simulator is £25 an hour indoors, and it is the busiest thing on site between November and February. Groups use it for course play, and golfers use it for measured practice when standing outside has stopped being appealing. It needs booking, particularly at weekends and across the Christmas period.

Your Body Is Not Doing What You Ask It To

Book TPI screening at £75. It is a physical assessment that separates the swing faults you can coach away from the ones your body is currently preventing, and it changes the coaching plan that follows. Follow-up work runs in the golf fitness suite, which is built around the golf swing rather than general fitness.

You Are Booking for a Child or Joining a Group

The junior academy covers ages six to sixteen, and the ladies academy runs women-only groups for beginners and improvers. Both work on blocks of sessions, so ring 07710 582036 or send an enquiry to find out when the next block starts and whether there is space in it.
