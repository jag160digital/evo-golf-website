Why Practice on Grass?

Mats feel different from real grass. Our grass driving range gives you authentic contact feel and course conditions, helping your practice translate better to actual golf. Combined with our Trackman range for data and analysis, you get both natural feel and swing feedback.

Authentic Turf Contact

Natural grass means different divot patterns and feedback than mats. Your hands feel how the club meets real turf.

Course Conditions

Practice on varied lies and grass densities, just like you'll face on a real course. Build adaptability and consistency.

Injury Prevention

Grass provides natural shock absorption and reduces joint stress compared to hard mat practice.

Feel Development

Develop your touch and course sense. Combine grass practice with Trackman data for complete feedback loop.

Grass Range + Trackman Technology

Get the best of both worlds. Practice on our grass range with the authentic feel of real turf, then use our Trackman bays for detailed swing data and analysis.
