● The movement side of golf

What Is SwingFit?

SwingFit is the strand of Evolution Golf Academy that deals with your body rather than your technique. It pairs TPI movement screening with golf specific physical work, so that the changes your coach asks for are changes your body can actually make.

It exists because of something the academy learned the hard way. Will Painter's own playing career stalled while he was working hard on his swing and getting nowhere, and the reason had nothing to do with technique. His body simply was not capable of consistently producing the movements he was trying to make. The answer was not the swing, it was the body.

Why Do Swing Changes Fail?

Most of the time, because they were never physically available in the first place. If your hips will not rotate far enough, you will find a way to make the turn anyway, and that way will be a compensation somewhere else. Coach the compensation and it moves rather than disappears.

This is the pattern behind an enormous number of golfers who have had lessons for years and not improved. They are not lazy and their coach is not incompetent. They are being asked for positions their body cannot reach, and no amount of repetition changes that.

What Does the Screening Actually Test?

The TPI screen is sixteen movement tests covering rotation, stability, mobility and balance. Each result is then linked to the swing characteristic it tends to produce - early extension, loss of posture, casting and the rest - so you finish with a clear picture of which of your swing faults are technical and which are physical.

● How it runs

How Does SwingFit Work?

Screen first, then train what the screen found. The order matters more than the exercises.

1

Screen

A TPI movement screen, sixteen tests covering rotation, stability, mobility and balance. About an hour, and no golf swing required for most of it.

2

Link

Every restriction found is matched to the swing characteristic it tends to cause, so you can see which of your faults are physical rather than technical.

3

Plan

A prioritised set of exercises targeting the restrictions that will make the biggest difference to your golf first, rather than a long list you will abandon in a fortnight.

4

Train

Work through it in the golf fitness suite or at home. Consistency beats intensity here by a distance.

5

Re-test

Screen again and re-measure clubhead speed on Trackman. If it worked the numbers move, and if it did not we change the plan rather than tell you to try harder.

● Coaching and movement together

How Does SwingFit Fit With Golf Lessons?

They are two halves of the same job. Your golf lesson works on what your swing does; SwingFit works on what your body will allow. Run them separately and they pull against each other.

Run together, your coach knows which positions are realistic to ask for right now and which need the body addressing first. That is the difference between a swing change that holds and one that quietly reverts over six weeks.

  • ✓Screen before rebuilding - know what is available before changing it
  • ✓Faster progress - lessons target what is actually fixable now
  • ✓Fewer injuries - compensations are what hurt backs and elbows
  • ✓Measured - Trackman before and after, not a feeling
  • ✓Suits every age - older golfers often gain the most
