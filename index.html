What Does a Golf Academy Near Derby & Nottingham Offer?

Whether you're picking up a club for the first time or sharpening a single-figure handicap, Evolution Golf Academy combines PGA Professional coaching, a Trackman Official Performance Centre and a full set of practice facilities - all in one place at Ormonde Fields Golf Club in Ripley, Derbyshire.

Beginner Coaching

Beginner golf lessons with a PGA Professional. No experience needed, clubs provided, and a clear path from your first swing to your first round on a golf course.
