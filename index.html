What Is a Golf Academy and How Is It Different From a Golf Club?

A golf club sells you access to a course. A golf academy sells you improvement. Evolution Golf Academy sits on the grounds of Ormonde Fields Golf Club on Nottingham Road in Codnor, Ripley, and everything on the site exists for a single purpose: to send you home a better golfer than you arrived. Everything here is open to the public, so you do not need to be a member to book anything. Membership exists if you want it, and it gives you priority booking and better rates, but nothing on the site is locked behind it.

That matters around Derby and Nottingham because serious coaching and serious practice technology have traditionally been locked behind private clubs. If you wanted one to one golf lessons with an Advanced PGA Professional and a launch monitor pointed at your swing, you generally had to know somebody, join something, or drive for the best part of an hour. We built this academy so that the drive is fifteen minutes from Derby and twenty from Nottingham, the parking is free, and the answer to "can I come tomorrow" is usually yes.

Who Is the Golf Academy For?

This golf academy in Derbyshire is for complete beginners who have never held a club. Children aged six to sixteen in the junior academy. Women starting out or coming back through the ladies academy. Mid handicappers who have shot the same score for three seasons and cannot work out why. Single figure players chasing the last two shots before a club championship. The facilities are identical for all of them. What changes is what the coach does with them once you are in the bay.

What Is Actually on the Site?

Seven Trackman-equipped bays on the Trackman driving range, two or more indoor golf simulators, a private Trackman teaching bay for lessons and video analysis, a grass driving range for the drier months, a short game area covering chipping, pitching, bunker play and putting, and a golf fitness suite for mobility, strength and speed work. Custom club fitting runs on Trackman data and our fittings are brand neutral, so you can buy from us or anywhere you like.

Do I Need to Be Any Good Before I Come?

No, and the most common thing we hear from first-time callers on 07710 582036 is that they want to get a bit better before they book a lesson. That is roughly equivalent to tidying the house before the cleaner arrives. Get Into Golf and structured beginner coaching exist precisely for golfers who feel that way, and nobody here has ever raised an eyebrow at a bad swing. We have all made them. You do not need your own clubs either: we can lend you a set for lessons, and the range and simulators include club hire at no extra cost.

What Makes Golf Coaching at This Academy Different?

Data and Feel, Rather Than One or the Other

There are coaches who will not look at a screen and coaches who will not look at you. Both are missing half the picture. Trackman tells us what the club and ball actually did, which stops a lesson turning into two people guessing. What it cannot tell us is what a change feels like in your hands, and that is the part that has to survive the drive home. Every session here uses both, and the numbers exist to settle the argument, not to run the lesson.

Plain English, With the Jargon Left in the Bag

Attack angle, dynamic loft and low point control are all real things, and none of them help a golfer who has come for an hour after work and wants to stop slicing. Will will happily go as deep into the technical detail as you want, and just as happily leave all of it out. If you finish a lesson unable to explain to your playing partner what you are working on, the lesson has not landed.

A Practice Plan You Can Realistically Do

A golf lesson without a practice plan is entertainment. Every golfer leaves knowing exactly what to hit, how many, and what a good outcome looks like when they next get to the Trackman driving range. If you want that structured across a season rather than an hour, the monthly coaching programme at £60 a month builds it for you and adjusts it as you improve.

The Same Coach, Session After Session

Improvement is cumulative and coaching relationships take a few sessions to find their rhythm. You work with Will Painter, an Advanced PGA Professional, from your first enquiry onward, so nobody is re-reading your swing from scratch every time you arrive. What changes between sessions is not the person in the bay, it is what the session is aimed at, because the plan is matched to your goals rather than to whoever has a gap in the diary. You can read how Will works on the meet the team page before you book your first 1:1 golf lesson at £55.

Equipment That Is Not Quietly Working Against You

A surprising number of stubborn faults turn out to be a shaft that is too soft, a lie angle that is two degrees out, or a driver with more loft than the swing needs. Custom fitting here runs on Trackman numbers rather than a chart on the wall, and our fittings are brand neutral, so you can buy from us or anywhere you like. Because the fitting sits alongside the coaching, the specification accounts for where your swing is heading rather than only where it is today. Ask us about a fitting if your clubs have never been checked.
