What Does the Monthly Golf Programme Include?

Sporadic lessons fix the fault of the week. A structured monthly programme fixes your game. For £60 per month you get regular PGA Professional coaching, a written plan and Trackman data tracking every stage of your progress.

Structured Plan

Your coach maps out where your game is, where it needs to be and the order to work in. Every session builds on the last instead of starting from scratch.

Is a Monthly Programme Better Than Single Golf Lessons?

For golfers who want measurable progress, yes. A single lesson treats the symptom you turned up with. A programme works through the causes in the right order, with Trackman data showing exactly what has changed. It is the most popular way to be coached at Evolution Golf Academy, from Derby, Nottingham and across the East Midlands.
