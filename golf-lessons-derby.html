Golf lessons Derby - FAQs

How far are you from Derby city centre?+
We're approximately 15 minutes from Derby city centre on the A610 towards Ripley. We're located at Nottingham Road, Codnor, Ripley, DE5 9RJ. Free parking is available on site.
Are there any other Trackman academies closer to Derby?+
Not with the same full setup - three PGA Professionals, 7 Trackman range bays, indoor simulators and a custom fitting suite all in one place. We're the most comprehensive golf facility this close to Derby.
What's the price for a lesson?+
Single lessons are £55 with a PGA Professional. Our monthly coaching programme is £60/month and is the most popular option for Derby golfers who want structured improvement.
Can I just turn up to use the range?+
Yes - the Trackman range is walk-in, no booking needed. Baskets start at £4. We're open 7am to 8pm every day.
