Where Can You Get Golf Lessons in Derby?

Derby is not short of places to hit a golf ball, but it is short of places to actually learn. Most options are either a members' club with a waiting list and a dress code, or a strip of mats with no coaching attached. Golf lessons in Derby at Evolution Golf Academy sit deliberately between the two: a full performance facility with PGA Professionals, open to anybody, with no membership required.

We are based at Ormonde Fields Golf Club in Codnor, just outside Ripley, which puts us roughly fifteen minutes from Derby city centre on the A610. For most Derby golfers that is a shorter journey than crossing the city in rush hour, and there is free parking when you arrive rather than a multi-storey and a walk.

What Makes This Different From a Normal Driving Range?

Every bay on our range is fitted with Trackman, the dual-radar launch monitor used on the PGA Tour. That means when you take a golf lesson with a PGA Professional, your coach is working from measured numbers rather than an opinion about what your ball flight looked like. Ball speed, launch angle, spin rate, carry distance, club path and face angle are all on screen within a second of impact.

It changes what a lesson can achieve. Instead of "that looked better", you get a number that either moved or did not. If a grip change adds four miles an hour of ball speed and straightens your club path by three degrees, you can see it happen, and so can your coach.

Do You Need to Be Any Good to Book a Lesson?

No, and this is the single most common thing Derby golfers ask before booking. We coach complete beginners every week. Beginner golf lessons assume no prior knowledge, clubs are provided, and nobody expects you to know what a lie angle is. If you have literally never held a club, Get Into Golf is an even gentler starting point.

● Choosing the right coaching

Which Golf Lessons Are Right for You?

If You Have Never Played Golf Before

Start with Get Into Golf or beginner golf lessons. Both assume nothing, provide clubs, and are built around getting you comfortable rather than technically perfect. Most Derby beginners are playing a short course within a few months.

If You Play but Have Stopped Improving

This is what the monthly coaching programme exists for. Occasional one-off lessons tend to produce a good week followed by a slow slide back. Structured coaching with a practice plan between sessions is what actually shifts a handicap.

If Your Body Is Fighting Your Swing

If you physically cannot get into a position, no amount of coaching will put you there. TPI screening assesses how your body moves and links each restriction to the swing fault it produces, and the golf fitness suite is where you then work on it.

If Your Clubs Might Be the Problem

Off-the-shelf clubs fit the average golfer, and almost nobody is average. Hayden Berridge PGA runs custom fitting using Trackman data, covering shaft, loft, lie and gapping, and you leave with a full specification document whether you buy from us or not.

If You Are Bringing a Child

The junior academy covers ages six to sixteen under Scott Hassall PGA. Plenty of Derby parents drive over for it because structured junior coaching is genuinely hard to find locally.
