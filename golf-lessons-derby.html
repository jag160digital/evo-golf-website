Golf Lessons in Derby - PGA Coaches 15 Minutes From the City Centre.

You do not need a club membership or a long drive to get proper coaching. Evolution Golf Academy sits just outside Derby at Ormonde Fields Golf Club in Codnor, giving Derby golfers PGA Professional golf tuition and a Trackman Official Performance Centre on their doorstep.

1:1 Golf Lessons

Sixty minutes with a PGA Professional, Trackman data and video analysis throughout. The fastest way to fix what is actually costing you shots.

Trackman Driving Range

Covered floodlit bays with ball speed, carry and shot shape on every swing. Walk in, no booking, from £4 a basket.

Indoor Golf Simulator

Play world-famous courses indoors on Trackman. A proper option for Derby golfers through the winter months.

TPI Screening & Fitting

Movement screening and Trackman-based custom club fitting, so your body and your equipment both stop holding you back.
