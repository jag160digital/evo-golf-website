Why Does the Short Game Matter Most?

Roughly half your shots happen within a hundred yards of the flag, yet almost nobody practises there. An hour on the short game area will take more strokes off your handicap than an hour smashing drivers ever will.

Putting Green

Work on pace, read and start line on a true surface. Three-putts are the cheapest shots in golf to eliminate.

Bunker Play

Practise the shot most golfers dread until it stops being a shot you dread. Technique here is very learnable.

Chipping & Pitching

Build a reliable chip and a controlled pitch. Distance control around the green is where handicaps genuinely fall.

Short Game Coaching

Take a lesson focused entirely on scoring shots. Our PGA coaches will tell you exactly where your strokes are going.

● Where handicaps actually fall

Practise the Shots That Save Strokes.

Most amateurs practise what they enjoy rather than what they need. A short game session is less glamorous than the driving range and considerably more effective - and it is available seven days a week here.

  • ✓Chipping and pitching - distance control from inside 50 yards
  • ✓Bunker technique - a repeatable method, not hope
  • ✓Putting - pace and start line on a true green
