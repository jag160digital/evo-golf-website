● Coaching for women, all abilities

What Are Ladies Golf Lessons Like Here?

A relaxed, welcoming space to learn. Friendly women-only golf lessons, from complete first-timers to club golfers.

The women-only part is the point, and it is worth saying plainly rather than burying it. Ladies academy sessions are for women only. No mixed group, no row of men waiting behind you and no working out whether you are allowed to be there. It is a genuine difference rather than a marketing line, and it is the single reason most of the women coached here booked in the first place.

Ladies golf lessons at the academy are the same standard of Advanced PGA Professional coaching we give anybody else, taught by the same coach, using the same Trackman data. The difference is context rather than content. Women taking up golf face a set of specific obstacles, most of them social rather than technical, and it is worth naming them rather than pretending they do not exist.

The obstacles are these. Golf clubs can feel closed, particularly if you do not already know a member. Equipment shops frequently assume a woman wants the shortest, lightest, pinkest set on the rack regardless of her height or swing speed. And there is a persistent, tiresome assumption that women want a gentler version of golf. We do not coach a gentler version of golf. We coach golf, and we start wherever you actually are.

Do I Need Any Experience to Book?

None. A significant share of the women we coach have never hit a golf ball, and clubs are provided so you can begin owning nothing. Equally, plenty of our ladies play off single figures and come in to work on wedge distance control or driver dispersion. Both are ordinary bookings here and neither gets a different quality of attention.

If you are starting from zero and would rather ease in, beginner coaching assumes no knowledge whatsoever, and Get Into Golf runs as a relaxed group where everybody present is equally new. If you already know you want focused work, book a 1:1 golf lesson and go straight at it.

Is This a Members Club?

No, and it is the point most worth making. Evolution Golf Academy is a coaching academy on the site of Ormonde Fields Golf Club, open to the public seven days a week from 7am to 8pm. There is no committee, no waiting list, no proposer and seconder, and no dress code that will catch you out. You book a session, you park for free, you have your lesson and you leave.

That also means you can practise here whenever you like. The Trackman driving range is walk-in from £4 a basket, the bays are covered and floodlit, and nobody is going to ask which club you belong to.

● What sessions cover

What Do Womens Golf Lessons Cover?

Whatever your game needs. These four are the most common reasons women book at the academy.

Starting From Nothing

Grip, stance, aim and a half swing, taught in a private bay with clubs provided. The aim of session one is that you strike some balls and enjoy the hour.

Ball Striking and Distance

Speed comes from sequencing and strike quality far more than from strength. Trackman shows exactly where yardage is being lost, which is rarely where people assume.

Short Game and Scoring

Chipping, pitching, bunkers and putting. The fastest available route to lower scores for any golfer, and the area most people practise least.

Clubs That Actually Fit

Shaft weight, flex, length and lie matched to your swing rather than to an assumption. Badly fitted clubs cost women more distance than almost anything else.

● From the bay to the first tee

How Do I Get Onto a Golf Course?

This is where a lot of women stall, and it is almost never about the swing. It is not knowing how a tee time works, whether you are playing too slowly, where to stand, or what happens when you top one off the first in front of people. Every one of those is answerable, and answering them is part of the coaching rather than an extra.

We take golfers out onto Ormonde Fields once contact is reliable, usually three or four holes rather than a full round. You learn pace of play, course management, the handful of etiquette points that genuinely matter, and how to keep a card. After that, playing with strangers stops being daunting.

● How to begin

How Do I Start Golf Lessons for Women?

Five straightforward steps, and you can stop at any of them without anybody chasing you.

1

Ring or book online

Call 07710 582036 or book a session online. Say what stage you are at, including if that stage is nothing at all, and we will match the session to it.

2

Turn up in trainers

No golf clothing needed and no equipment required for a first session. Free parking on site at Ormonde Fields Golf Club, Nottingham Road, Codnor.

3

Have your swing measured

Trackman records what actually happens rather than what anyone assumes. That matters especially for women, because generic advice about distance is usually built around a male swing speed.

4

Work on one thing at a time

One change per session, with a practice plan you can do in twenty minutes on the range. Four swing thoughts at once helps nobody.

5

Get out on the course

A few holes with a coach, then nine, then eighteen if you want it. Some women never play a full round and simply enjoy practising, which is a perfectly good way to have golf in your life.

● Straight answers

Questions Women Ask Most Often.

Will I Be the Only Woman There?

No, because ladies academy group sessions are women-only. Everybody in the room is a woman and the abilities run from complete first-timers to club golfers, so there is no version of this where you are the odd one out. The academy also coaches plenty of women in one to one lessons and on the monthly programme, and the range is used by women on their own every single day.

Do I Need Womens Clubs?

You need clubs that fit you, which is a different question. Standard womens sets are built for an average that many women are not: too short for taller players, too light for stronger ones, too flexible for faster swings. A brand neutral fitting session on Trackman measures your actual swing speed and delivery, and the spec comes from that, so you can buy from us or anywhere you like. See custom fitting for what that involves.

How Do I Add Distance?

Strike quality first, then sequencing, then speed. Most golfers lose more yardage to hitting the toe or the heel than to a lack of clubhead speed, and smash factor on Trackman shows that within a handful of shots. Once the strike is central, speed work through the golf fitness suite can add genuine yards, and a TPI screening tells us whether rotation is the limiting factor.

Can I Bring a Friend?

Yes, and it makes the practice happen. Two people booking together tend to keep going where one drifts off. Couples often start on Get Into Golf together, and mothers frequently book their own session alongside their child's junior academy slot so the trip does double duty.

Women travel to the academy from Derby, Nottingham, Ripley, Belper, Heanor and across Derbyshire and Nottinghamshire, largely because it is fifteen minutes from Derby on the A610 with free parking. If you would rather ask something before booking, the contact page gets you a straight answer, and the FAQs cover the practical detail.
