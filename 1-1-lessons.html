What Do 1:1 Golf Lessons Include?

Every 1:1 golf lesson at Evolution Golf Academy is a full hour with a PGA Professional, built around Trackman data, video analysis and a personal plan - so every session works from facts, not guesswork, whatever part of your game needs it most.

Trackman Data

Ball speed, launch angle, carry distance and shot shape on every single shot. You see exactly what changed when you made an adjustment - improvement you can measure, not just feel.

Video Analysis

Your swing filmed and broken down side by side with your coach, then sent to you to keep. Review your lesson at home and know exactly what to work on between sessions.
