Indoor golf simulator near Derby & Nottingham

Our Trackman simulator is at Ormonde Fields Golf Club, Codnor, on the Derby to Nottingham road. Around 20 minutes from Derby, 25 from Nottingham, and the closest simulator of its kind for golfers in Ripley, Heanor and Ilkeston.

Play full rounds on famous courses, work on your game with complete shot data, or compete with friends. Up to four players can share a bay, and the more of you there are, the cheaper it gets per person.

Because it is indoors, weather never cancels your golf. Golfers from across Derby, Nottingham and Derbyshire book the simulator through winter to keep playing while the courses are wet.

How Does a Golf Simulator Actually Work?

You hit a real golf ball with your own clubs into a screen a few yards away. Trackman's radar tracks the club through impact and the ball through the first part of its flight, then models the rest from the measured launch conditions: ball speed, launch angle, spin rate, spin axis and attack angle. Because it measures rather than guesses, a thin three iron looks like a thin three iron on screen, and a well struck one runs out the way it should.

That accuracy is what separates a Trackman bay from the camera based systems in most bars and entertainment venues. It is the same dual radar technology used on the PGA Tour and on the range at major championships, which is why the carry numbers you see indoors match the ones you get outside on a still day.

Is It the Same Technology as the Driving Range Bays?

It is the same launch monitor and the same measurements you see in the Trackman driving range bays, doing a different job. The range is about volume and immediate feedback. The simulator wraps that data in a golf course, a target and a score, which changes how you behave. Golfers concentrate differently when there is water short of the green, even an imaginary one.

That makes the simulator the better choice for pressure practice and for playing with friends, and the range the better choice for working through the bag. Most regulars use both. If you want the numbers explained rather than simply displayed, that is a coaching conversation, and it happens next door in the Trackman teaching bay with an Advanced PGA Professional.

● Top questions from golfers

Trackman simulator Derby & Nottingham FAQs

Do I need to book the simulator?+
Yes. The simulator is booked by the hour online so your bay is guaranteed when you arrive. Evenings and weekends fill up first through winter, so book ahead if you want a peak slot.
How much does it cost?+
Off peak, from £13 per hour for one player up to £24 for four, which works out at £6 each. Peak is a fixed £25 per hour for up to four players. The price is per bay, so splitting it between friends is always the cheapest way to play.
How many people can play?+
Up to four golfers per bay, which is ideal for a group round or a competition with friends. Rounds take longer with four, so most groups book two hours if they want to finish eighteen holes.
Do I use my own clubs?+
Yes, real clubs and real balls into the impact screen. Bring your own if you have them, and if you do not, ask us about clubs when you book and we will sort it.
What courses can I play?+
A large library of famous courses, including links classics and major championship venues, plus practice ranges and a set of Trackman games and challenges for when you do not fancy a full round.
Is it good for beginners?+
Very. The data and the relaxed indoor setting make it a great place to start, with nobody watching and no pressure to keep up with a group behind. Lessons can be taken in the bay too, so a beginner can learn and play in the same place.
