● Indoor golf near Derby & Nottingham

What Is a Trackman Golf Simulator?

A Trackman golf simulator combines dual-radar ball tracking with a full course simulation, so the shot you hit indoors behaves exactly as it would outdoors. It is the same technology tour players use to dial in their numbers - and it means a wet Tuesday in February is still a golf day.

Play World-Famous Courses

Tee it up on courses you would otherwise never get on, without leaving Derbyshire. Play a full round, or just the holes that interest you.

Tour-Level Accuracy

Trackman is the launch monitor standard on tour. Every carry number, curve and spin rate is measured, not estimated.

Practice Modes & Games

Beyond playing rounds, use the simulator for targeted practice, closest-to-the-pin challenges and long drive competitions.

Private Bays

Your own enclosed bay. Bring a friend or three, play at your own pace, no queue behind you and no pressure.

● Who the simulator suits

Who Is the Golf Simulator Suitable For?

Just about everyone. The indoor golf simulator works as a serious practice tool for low handicappers, a comfortable first introduction for complete beginners, and a genuinely good night out for a group who fancy something different.

  • ✓Club golfers - practise with real numbers through the winter
  • ✓Beginners - learn indoors without an audience or a wet fairway
  • ✓Groups and friends - play a round together in a couple of hours
  • ✓Anyone short on daylight - full rounds at 7am or 7pm, all year
