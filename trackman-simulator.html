Simulator FAQs

Do I need to book in advance?+
Yes - simulator bays need to be booked ahead. Call 07710 582036 or fill in the contact form and we'll confirm your slot. We're open 7am to 8pm every day.
How many people can use a simulator bay at once?+
Our simulator bays comfortably accommodate a group of 2-4 players for a round of golf. For larger corporate groups, get in touch and we can discuss options.
What courses are available?+
200+ courses are available in the Trackman system including Pebble Beach, St Andrews, Augusta National, TPC Sawgrass, Royal Birkdale and many more. The library is regularly updated.
Is the simulator good for practice as well as fun?+
Absolutely. It's Trackman, so every shot gives you the same data as a coaching session - ball speed, spin, launch angle, shot shape. Many of our members use sim time specifically for practice rather than course play.
Can I book a coaching session in the simulator?+
Yes - this is one of the best ways to learn. Our PGA coaches regularly run lessons in the simulator bays using the Trackman data as the basis for the session. Book a coaching session and specify the simulator.
