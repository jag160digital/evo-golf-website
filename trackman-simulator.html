What Is a Trackman Golf Simulator?

A Trackman golf simulator is an indoor golf system that tracks every shot using the same dual-radar technology used by PGA Tour professionals and the world's leading golf coaches. It measures ball speed, spin rate, launch angle, carry distance and shot shape to a level of accuracy that outdoor driving ranges cannot match.

At Evolution Golf Academy, our golf simulators near Derby and Nottingham let you play a full round on over 200 of the world's most famous courses - Pebble Beach, St Andrews, Augusta National, TPC Sawgrass - with genuine Trackman data on every shot. You are not just playing a game. You are improving your actual golf swing.

Good for groups, genuinely useful for practice, and good fun on a Tuesday evening in January when the outdoor range is not an option.

Indoor Golf Simulator Derby & Nottingham - FAQs

Do I need to book the golf simulator in advance?+
Yes - simulator bays need to be booked ahead. Call 07710 582036 or fill in the contact form and we will confirm your slot. We are open 7am to 8pm every day.
How many people can use the golf simulator at once?+
Our simulator bays comfortably accommodate 2-4 players for a round of golf. For larger corporate groups near Derby or Nottingham, get in touch and we can discuss options.
What golf courses are available on the Trackman simulator?+
200+ courses are available including Pebble Beach, St Andrews, Augusta National, TPC Sawgrass, Royal Birkdale and many more. The Trackman course library is regularly updated.
Is the golf simulator good for practice as well as playing?+
Absolutely. It is Trackman, so every shot gives you the same data as a golf coaching session - ball speed, spin, launch angle, shot shape. Many golfers near Derby and Nottingham use simulator time specifically for practice rather than course play.
Can I book a golf coaching session in the simulator?+
Yes - and it is one of the most effective ways to improve your game. Our PGA coaches regularly run lessons in the simulator bays using the Trackman data as the basis for the entire session. Book a coaching session and specify the simulator.
