What the junior programme covers

Technique foundations

Grip, stance, swing path and ball striking built correctly from the start. Good habits at 8 years old are still good habits at 18.

Course play

Rules, etiquette, scoring and how to think around a golf course. Juniors learn to play golf, not just hit balls on a range.

Trackman feedback

The same technology used with adult players. Juniors quickly grasp the connection between what they do and what the data shows.

Junior Academy FAQs

What age range does the junior academy cover?+
We coach juniors from age 6 to 16. Different age groups receive age-appropriate coaching - a 6-year-old's session looks quite different from a 15-year-old's. Get in touch and we'll confirm the right programme for your child.
Does my child need their own clubs?+
Not to start. We have clubs available for initial sessions. When the time comes to invest in their own set, Hayden can do a junior fitting to make sure the clubs actually suit their swing and height.
Are sessions individual or group?+
Both options are available. Scott runs group junior sessions as well as individual lessons. For juniors who want to progress quickly, individual or small-group sessions are the most effective.
How do I enrol my child?+
Call 07710 582036 or fill in the contact form and ask for junior coaching with Scott Hassall. We'll confirm session availability and the right programme for your child's age and experience.
