What Do Junior Golf Lessons Include?

Golf swing foundations

Grip, stance, swing path and ball striking built correctly from day one. Good technique at 8 years old is still good technique at 18 - and bad habits formed early take years to undo.

Course play and etiquette

Rules, etiquette, scoring and how to think around a golf course. Junior golfers learn to play golf - not just hit balls on a driving range.

Trackman data feedback

The same launch monitor technology used with adults. Juniors quickly understand the link between what they do and what the data shows - which makes progress faster.

Junior Golf Lessons Derby & Nottingham - FAQs

What age can a child start junior golf lessons?+
We coach junior golfers from age 6 to 16. Different age groups get age-appropriate sessions - a 6-year-old's lesson looks quite different to a 15-year-old's. Get in touch and we'll confirm the right programme for your child.
Do kids need their own clubs for golf lessons?+
Not to start. We have clubs available for initial sessions. When the time comes to invest in their own set, Hayden can do a junior custom fitting to make sure the clubs match your child's swing and height - off-the-shelf junior clubs are often the wrong specification.
Are junior golf lessons one to one or in a group?+
Both options are available. Scott runs group junior sessions as well as individual lessons. For junior golfers who want to progress quickly, individual or small-group sessions are most effective.
How do I book junior golf lessons near Derby or Nottingham?+
Call 07710 582036 or fill in the contact form and ask for junior coaching with Scott Hassall. We'll confirm session availability and the right programme for your child's age and experience level.
