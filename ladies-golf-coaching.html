What Do Ladies Golf Lessons Include?

Your first session

No assessment, no test, no group of strangers watching you swing. Your first session is about where you are, what you want, and starting in the right direction. Nothing assumed.

Whether you want a one-off lesson or a structured monthly programme, coaching is built around your schedule and your goals. No two golfers are at the same place in their game.

Ladies Golf Lessons Derby & Nottingham - FAQs

Do I need experience to book ladies golf lessons?+
None at all. We coach complete beginners regularly. Your coach starts exactly where you are and builds from there. No prior knowledge assumed, no experience needed.
Are ladies golf lessons separate from men's?+
We offer individual ladies lessons and group options. Get in touch and we'll match you to the right session type based on your goals and preference.
Do I need my own clubs for ladies golf lessons?+
Not for your first session. We have clubs you can use. When you're ready to invest in your own set, a custom fitting with Hayden is worth doing - ladies clubs off the shelf are often the wrong specification for most women's swing speeds.
How do I book ladies golf lessons near Derby or Nottingham?+
Call 07710 582036 or fill in the contact form. We're open 7am to 8pm every day and can usually get you in within a few days.
