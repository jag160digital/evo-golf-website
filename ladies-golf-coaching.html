Golf coaching for women - without the intimidation

A lot of women who want to start golf are put off by the club environment. The assumption that you should already know what you're doing. The slightly unwelcoming atmosphere that still hangs around certain clubs and ranges.

That's not what you'll find here. Our academy was built on the principle that high-quality coaching shouldn't only be available to a certain type of golfer. Ladies coaching sessions at EGA are built around what you want to achieve, at your own pace, with PGA-qualified coaches who know how to teach - not just how to play.

Beginners start from scratch and leave with something that works. Improvers get specific, data-driven feedback on what's holding their score back.

Ladies coaching FAQs

Do I need any experience to book a ladies lesson?+
None at all. We coach complete beginners regularly. Your coach will start exactly where you are and build from there. No experience needed, no prior knowledge assumed.
Are ladies lessons mixed or separate from men's?+
We offer both individual ladies lessons and group options. Get in touch and we'll match you to the right session type based on your goals and preference.
Do I need my own clubs?+
Not for your first session. We have clubs you can use. When you're ready to invest in your own set, a custom fitting with Hayden is worth doing - ladies clubs off the shelf are often the wrong specification for most female golfers' swing speeds.
How do I book?+
Call 07710 582036 or fill in the contact form. We're open 7am to 8pm every day and can usually get you in within a few days.
