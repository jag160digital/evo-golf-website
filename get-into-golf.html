Get Into Golf - Play in Days

Get Into Golf is a fast-track introduction programme. Designed for complete beginners, you'll learn the essentials and be playing on a real golf course within weeks.

Grip & Setup

Proper grip, stance and posture from lesson one. These fundamentals are essential for every golfer.

Basic Swing

Simple, effective swing technique you can practice immediately. Build consistency and confidence with every session.

Course Ready

Etiquette, course management and practical tips for playing your first 9 holes. Feel prepared and confident on the course.
