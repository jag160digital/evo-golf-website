How Do I Get Into Golf as a Complete Beginner?

You turn up. That is genuinely the whole entry requirement. Get Into Golf sessions are the friendly front door to the game - no equipment, no experience and no dress code needed, just a PGA Professional who starts from the very beginning.

Clubs Provided

Everything you need is here - clubs, balls and a covered bay. Come in trainers and comfortable clothes. Buying equipment comes later, if you want it to.

Why Start Golf at Evolution Golf Academy?

A lot of people who want to try golf are put off by the traditional club environment - the assumption that you should already know what you are doing. That is not what you will find here. Our coaches are PGA Professionals who know how to teach, not just how to play, and beginners from Derby, Nottingham and across Derbyshire start from scratch every week.

  • ✓Zero judgement - everyone in the session is new to the game too
  • ✓Covered, floodlit bays - sessions run whatever the weather
  • ✓Trackman feedback - see your progress after every single swing
  • ✓Open 7 days - 7am to 8pm, easy to fit around work and family
