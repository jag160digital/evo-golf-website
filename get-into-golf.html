What Is Get Into Golf?

Get Into Golf is the relaxed, welcoming entry point into the game. Clubs are provided, no experience is needed and nothing is assumed about what you already know. If you have never held a golf club in your life, this is the session written for you.

It is a group starting point for adults who have never played, or who played a handful of times years ago and would like another attempt without any pressure. It runs with an Advanced PGA Professional, clubs are provided, and the format is deliberately sociable. Everybody in the group is at the same stage, which removes the single biggest thing that puts new golfers off.

Golf has an image problem, and a lot of it is deserved. People assume they need expensive equipment, a membership, the right clothes and a working knowledge of etiquette before they are allowed near a ball. None of that is required here. Evolution Golf Academy is a coaching academy, open to the public, seven days a week from 7am to 8pm, and the range and short game area are open to anyone who turns up.

Is a Group Better Than a One to One Lesson?

For a nervous beginner, often yes. A group is cheaper, more relaxed, and there is a pleasant kind of cover in not being the only person hitting a bad shot. You also learn from watching other people make the same mistakes, which is more useful than it sounds. A one to one session is faster and more targeted, so plenty of people start in a group and move to 1:1 golf lessons once the nerves have gone.

There is no wrong answer. If you already know you want to take golf seriously, go straight to beginner coaching and skip the group. If you are curious but not committed, and the thought of an hour alone with a coach is off-putting, this is the one to book.

What Does Golf Actually Cost to Start?

Less than most people assume, provided you do not begin by buying a full set. Clubs are provided for beginner sessions, a basket of range balls starts from £4, and a one to one lesson is £55 if and when you want one. You do not need to be a member to practise here or to be coached here, which is the largest cost most people wrongly think is compulsory.

When you do want your own clubs, a sensible second-hand set will get you a long way, and your coach can tell you what to look for. Later on, custom fitting on Trackman makes sense, though there is no rush and no obligation to buy anything from us at all.

Common Golf Beginner Worries - Answered.

These four come up in almost every conversation with a new golfer. All four are entirely reasonable and all four have a short answer.

I Will Look Stupid

Everyone in the group is starting at the same point, and the coach has seen every conceivable air shot. Nobody is watching you, because they are all busy worrying about their own swing.

I Have No Equipment

Clubs are provided. Wear trainers with a bit of grip and something you can turn in. Buying a set before you know whether you enjoy golf is a genuinely bad idea.

I Do Not Know the Rules

You need roughly five etiquette basics to play with strangers comfortably, and they take five minutes to explain. The rulebook can wait, possibly forever.

Golf Clubs Are Snobby

Some are. This is an academy rather than a members club, so there is no committee, no dress code to fall foul of and no need to be introduced by anybody.

Small groups, an Advanced PGA Professional, and an hour spent hitting balls rather than being lectured. The coach covers grip, stance and aim in plain English, then gets everybody swinging quickly, because standing around listening is how people decide golf is boring.

How Do I Start Playing Golf?

Five steps, spread over a few months rather than a few weeks. Nobody needs to rush any of them.

1

Come and hit some balls

Turn up at the walk-in range with £4 for a basket, borrow a club and see whether you enjoy it. No booking, no commitment and no conversation with anybody required.

2

Join a Get Into Golf session

Learn grip, stance and aim with a PGA coach alongside other beginners. Ring 07710 582036 to find out when the next group runs and what it costs.

3

Build a swing you can repeat

A few sessions of beginner coaching or the monthly programme takes you from making contact to making contact reliably, which is the point golf becomes fun.

4

Learn the short game

Chipping and putting are where beginners score, and both are easier to learn than the full swing. An hour on the practice green saves more shots than an hour with a driver.

5

Play a few holes

Not eighteen. Three or four holes with a coach or a patient friend, learning pace of play and where to stand. Ormonde Fields is on the same site, so the first tee is a short walk away.

● Honest answers

Is Golf Worth Taking Up?

How Much Time Does Golf Really Take?

Less than the eighteen hole stereotype suggests. Nine holes takes around two hours and is a perfectly respectable round. A range session can be twenty minutes. Plenty of golfers at the academy never play a full round and simply enjoy practising, which is a legitimate way to use the game. The four hour Saturday commitment is a choice, not an entry requirement.

Do I Need to Be Fit or Sporty?

No. Golf is one of the few games where technique and timing beat athleticism, which is why people keep improving into their sixties and seventies. If you do have a physical restriction, a TPI screening identifies it and the swing is built around it. The golf fitness suite is there if you want to widen those limits, but it is optional.

What If I Am Coming Back After Years Away?

Very common, and usually easier than starting from scratch, because the pattern comes back faster than you expect. What tends to have gone is flexibility and speed rather than skill. A single session on Trackman will show you what has changed, and the honest answer is often that the swing is fine and the equipment is twenty years old.

Can I Bring a Friend or Partner?

Yes, and it is a good idea. Learning alongside somebody makes the practice happen and the sessions more enjoyable. Couples often start together and then split into individual lessons later once they progress at different rates, which they invariably do. Women who would prefer a female-friendly group should look at the ladies academy, and there is a junior academy for children aged 6 to 16.

Beginners come to us from Derby, Nottingham, Ripley, Codnor, Heanor and across Derbyshire and Nottinghamshire, largely because the A610 makes the academy easy to reach and parking is free. If you have questions before booking anything, the contact page and the FAQs cover most of them.
