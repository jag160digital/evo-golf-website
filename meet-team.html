● The coaching team

PGA Professional Golf Coaches in Derbyshire.

Three resident PGA Professionals coach at the academy - no assistants, no students. Between them they cover every level of golfer, from a first-ever lesson to single-figure handicaps, plus custom club fitting and a full junior programme for players from Derby, Nottingham and the surrounding towns.

Will Painter

Head Coach and Academy Director - PGA Professional

Will leads the coaching programme at the academy and is the first coach most new members meet. A PGA Professional specialising in technique, swing mechanics and long-term player development, he coaches every level - from a nervous first lesson to a single-figure golfer chasing a number. Sessions work from Trackman data and video, so you leave knowing exactly what changed and why.

Hayden Berridge

Coach and Custom Fitter - PGA Professional

Hayden is a PGA Professional who splits his time between coaching and custom club fitting built on Trackman data. Because he coaches as well as fits, he understands how equipment and swing interact - so a fitting with Hayden accounts for where your game is heading, not just where it is today. Lessons and fittings both finish with a clear, written plan.

Scott Hassall

Coach and Head of Juniors - PGA Professional

Scott is a PGA Professional who leads the Junior Academy, coaching players aged 6 to 16 alongside his adult coaching. He builds technique and confidence from the ground up, with structured group and individual sessions that keep young golfers improving - and enjoying it - week after week.

● How we coach

What Is Our Coaching Philosophy?

Data plus feel. Every golf lesson at the academy starts with Trackman numbers and video, so we know exactly what your swing is doing - then the coaching translates that into feels you can actually take to the course. No jargon, no rebuild for the sake of it, no drills you cannot repeat on your own.

  • ✓Facts first - Trackman measures every shot, so nothing is guessed
  • ✓Plain English - you leave knowing what changed and why, without the jargon
  • ✓Your game, your goals - coaching built around what you want, not a template
  • ✓Swing videos to keep - review your lesson at home between sessions
