Starting golf can feel overwhelming. It doesn't have to.

Most beginners put off their first lesson because they don't know what to expect - or they're worried about looking foolish. We hear that a lot. The truth is, every single golfer at this academy was a beginner once, including our coaches.

Your first lesson with Will Painter is built around making you feel comfortable, giving you something that actually works, and making sure you leave with a clear picture of what to practice next.

What your first lessons cover

We build from the foundations up. No shortcuts, no bad habits that'll need fixing later.

The setup

Grip, stance, alignment and posture. The things most beginners get wrong because nobody explained them properly. We fix this first - everything else builds on it.

Ball striking

How to make clean contact consistently. You'll see your data on Trackman - ball speed, launch angle, shot shape - so you can see the difference as you improve, not just feel it.

Course management

You don't need a perfect swing to play golf. Will teaches you how to think around a course so you're enjoying proper rounds sooner rather than later.

Beginner coaching - FAQs

Do I need to bring my own clubs?+
No. We have clubs you can borrow for your first session. If you're serious about continuing, we'd recommend looking at custom fitting with Hayden so your first set actually suits your swing.
How many lessons will I need before I can play on a course?+
It varies, but most beginners on our monthly programme are ready to play their first proper round within 3-4 months. The structured programme accelerates this significantly compared to the occasional single lesson.
What should I wear?+
Comfortable sports clothing and trainers are fine for a lesson. You don't need golf shoes for indoor bays. We're covered, so weather isn't a factor.
Is there a minimum age for adult lessons?+
No minimum age for adults. Junior coaching (ages 6-16) is run separately by Scott Hassall through the junior academy.
How far are you from Derby and Nottingham?+
We're on Nottingham Road in Codnor, Ripley - approximately 15 minutes from Derby city centre and 20 minutes from Nottingham. There's free parking on site.
