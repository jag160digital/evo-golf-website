Where Can You Get Golf Lessons Near Nottingham?

Nottingham golfers have a decent choice of courses and a much thinner choice of proper coaching facilities. Golf lessons near Nottingham at Evolution Golf Academy give you a Trackman Official Performance Centre with PGA Professionals on site, twenty minutes out of the city on the A610 through Eastwood.

The run out is straightforward. You leave Nottingham heading north west, pass through Eastwood, and the academy is on Nottingham Road at Ormonde Fields Golf Club in Codnor. No motorway, no city centre parking, and free parking when you get here. For golfers in Kimberley, Nuthall, Watnall or Eastwood it is often under fifteen minutes.

What Is a Trackman Performance Centre?

Trackman is the dual-radar launch monitor used on the PGA Tour and by most of the world's leading coaches. Being an Official Performance Centre means every bay here is fitted with it, so a golf lesson is built on measured data rather than a coach's impression of your ball flight.

In practice, that means you and your coach can see ball speed, spin rate, launch angle, carry distance, club path and face angle for every shot. When a change works you know immediately, because the numbers move. When it does not, you have not wasted six weeks finding out.

Is It Worth Driving Out of Nottingham for a Lesson?

That depends what you want from coaching. If you want somebody to watch you hit balls and offer a tip, you can find that closer to home. If you want your swing measured, your equipment checked and a plan you can follow between sessions, the twenty minutes buys you a facility that does not really exist inside the city.

● Choosing the right coaching

Which Golf Lessons Should Nottingham Golfers Book?

Complete Beginners

Beginner golf lessons assume no experience whatsoever and clubs are provided, so there is nothing to buy first. If the idea of a one to one still feels daunting, Get Into Golf runs as a friendlier group format.

Golfers Stuck on the Same Handicap

The monthly programme is built for this. One-off lessons tend to give you a good fortnight before old habits reassert themselves. Structured coaching with practice work between sessions is what moves a handicap and keeps it moved.

Anyone Who Suspects Their Body Is the Limit

If you cannot physically make a turn, coaching cannot give you one. TPI screening tests how your body actually moves and connects each restriction to the swing characteristic it produces, then the golf fitness suite is where you address it.

Golfers Playing With the Wrong Clubs

Custom fitting with Hayden Berridge PGA uses Trackman data to match shaft, loft, lie and gapping to your actual swing. You get a full written specification and no obligation to buy anything from us.

Winter Golfers

The indoor simulator and the covered floodlit Trackman range are the reason a lot of Nottingham golfers keep their game through the off season rather than starting again every April.
