● Where we are

Where Is Evolution Golf Academy Located?

Evolution Golf Academy is on Nottingham Road in Codnor, Ripley, right between Derby and Nottingham. The full address is Ormonde Fields Golf Club, Nottingham Road, Codnor, Ripley DE5 9RJ, in the middle of Derbyshire and a couple of minutes off the A610. Codnor sits between Ripley and Langley Mill, so if you know the road that runs from the Ripley bypass down towards Eastwood, you already know how to find us. The entrance is signed from Nottingham Road and the car park is immediately inside.

Being here rather than inside either city was a deliberate choice. A golf academy near Derby and Nottingham only works if both sets of golfers can get to it after work without resenting the journey, and the A610 does that job better than anything running through a city centre. Fifteen minutes from Derby, twenty five from Nottingham, and neither trip involves a park and ride or a parking charge.

Why Does Sitting Between Derby and Nottingham Work So Well?

Because the traffic runs the other way. Most evening congestion around the East Midlands is people heading into or out of the two city centres, and coming here you are travelling across that flow rather than with it. It also means one academy serves both counties in full, which is why we keep separate pages explaining the journey and coaching options for golf lessons from Derby and golf lessons from Nottingham.

What Postcode Should I Put in the Satnav?

DE5 9RJ. Some older satnavs place that postcode slightly short of the entrance, so if the arrival announcement fires before you see the sign, keep going for a few hundred metres along Nottingham Road. Searching for Ormonde Fields Golf Club or Evolution Golf Academy in Google Maps will take you to the correct entrance every time, and the map on this page is set to the same spot.

Is There Parking at the Golf Academy?

Yes, parking is free and on site, and there is normally space even at the busiest times on a weekend morning. You park once and everything is a short walk away, including the Trackman driving range, the teaching bays and the short game area. There is no barrier, no ticket machine and nothing to display in the windscreen.

● Getting here

How Do I Get to the Golf Academy From Derby and Nottingham?

Five routes covering where most golfers travel in from. All of them finish in the same free car park on Nottingham Road.

1

Driving from Derby, about 15 minutes

Head north east out of Derby on the A61 towards Ripley, then join the A610 towards Codnor and Langley Mill. Come off at Codnor and follow Nottingham Road. The entrance to Ormonde Fields is on your right. Full detail for golfers travelling this way is on our golf lessons Derby page.

2

Driving from Nottingham, about 25 minutes

Take the A610 north west out of Nottingham through Nuthall, Kimberley and Eastwood, continue past Langley Mill and turn off for Codnor. Nottingham Road runs straight to us. It is dual carriageway for most of the way, which is why the trip is quicker than the map suggests. See the golf lessons Nottingham page for more.

3

Coming off the M1 at Junction 26

Leave the M1 at Junction 26 and take the A610 north west towards Ripley. Stay on it past Eastwood and Langley Mill, then come off for Codnor and pick up Nottingham Road. It is about twelve minutes from the motorway roundabout, which makes the academy easy to reach from Sheffield, Chesterfield and Leicester as well.

4

Coming from Belper, Alfreton and Heanor

From Belper take the A610 east through Ambergate and Ripley. From Alfreton drop south on the A38 and A61 into Ripley, then follow signs for Codnor. From Heanor it is a straight run north on the A6007 and Codnor Denby Lane. All three journeys are inside fifteen minutes outside rush hour.

5

Parking and finding your way round the site

Turn in at Ormonde Fields Golf Club and park in the free car park directly ahead. The academy building and the covered range bays are signed from there. If it is your first visit, walk into reception and someone will point you at the right bay, whether you are here for a lesson or just picking up a basket for the driving range.

● Opening hours and parking

When Is the Golf Academy Open?

The academy is open Monday to Sunday, 7:00am to 8:00pm, every week of the year. That is the same on a Sunday as it is on a Wednesday, and the same in December as in June, because the range bays are covered and floodlit. Early mornings before work and evenings after it are both live coaching slots, which is the whole reason the window is that wide.

Opening hours

  • Monday to Sunday, 7:00am to 8:00pm, including weekends and bank holidays
  • Trackman driving range is walk-in, first come first served, no booking
  • Golf lessons, private bays, the simulator and TPI screening are booked in advance
  • Last range balls go out in time to be hit before 8pm, so allow yourself an hour

Parking

  • Free on-site parking at Ormonde Fields Golf Club, directly inside the entrance
  • No barrier, no ticket machine, no time limit and nothing to display
  • Level ground between the car park, the bays and the academy building
  • Space is normally available even on a Saturday morning

If you are visiting for the first time and want to check something before you set off, ring 07710 582036 or use the contact page and we answer within a working day. Full coaching options and prices are on the about page, and the town by town journeys are covered on the Ripley, Heanor and Belper pages.

● What is on site

What Will You Find When You Get Here?

The academy is bigger than it looks from Nottingham Road. Everything sits on one site, so a session can move from a lesson bay to a practice bay to the chipping green without anyone moving a car, and that continuity is a large part of why swing changes made here actually hold up on the course.

It is also built for a Derbyshire winter. The range bays are covered and floodlit until 8pm, and the indoor simulator bays carry on regardless of what is falling out of the sky, so the practice season does not stop in October like it does almost everywhere else.
