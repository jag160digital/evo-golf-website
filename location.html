Getting Here

Evolution Golf Academy is situated at Ormonde Fields Golf Club in Ripley, Derbyshire - a convenient location for golfers from Derby, Nottingham and across the East Midlands.

From Derby (15 minutes)

Head north on the A610 towards Ripley. Follow signs for Codnor and Ormonde Fields Golf Club. Plenty of parking available on site.

Directions from Derby

From Nottingham (20 minutes)

Take the A610 south towards Ripley. Follow signs for Codnor and Ormonde Fields Golf Club. Easy access from Nottingham city centre.
