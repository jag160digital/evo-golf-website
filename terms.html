● Booking

What Applies When You Book With Us?

This page is a working draft. It should be reviewed against your actual data handling before you rely on it.

These terms cover bookings made with Evolution Golf Academy at Ormonde Fields Golf Club, Nottingham Road, Codnor, Ripley DE5 9RJ. By booking a lesson, a simulator bay, a fitting or a screening you agree to them.

Booking and Payment

Lessons, simulator bays, custom fitting and TPI screening are booked in advance. The Trackman driving range is walk-in and does not need booking. Prices are confirmed at the time of booking.

Cancellations and Rescheduling

Let us know as far ahead as you reasonably can on 07710 582036 and we will move your session. Late cancellations are harder to reschedule because the coaching slot has usually gone. If we have to cancel, we will offer an alternative time.

Membership

Membership is optional and gives priority booking and better rates. Nothing at the academy requires membership. Current terms are confirmed when you join.

● Using the academy

What Are the Rules for Using the Facilities?

Safety

Golf clubs and golf balls are dangerous when people are careless. Stay in your bay, keep behind anyone who is swinging, and supervise children at all times. Follow any instruction from academy staff immediately, particularly on the grass range and in the short game area where people are moving about.

Equipment

Club hire on the range and simulators is included at no extra cost. Hired clubs remain ours, and you are responsible for returning them in the condition you received them. Damage caused by misuse may be charged for.

Your Own Belongings

Bring what you need but keep an eye on it. We cannot accept responsibility for personal property left unattended on site or in the car park.

Health

Golf, and golf fitness work in particular, is physical. Tell your coach about any injury, condition or medication that might be relevant before you start. We are not a medical service, and anything painful or persistent should be seen by a physiotherapist or GP.

Behaviour

We want an academy where a complete beginner feels as comfortable as a low handicapper. Behaviour that undermines that, towards staff or other golfers, means being asked to leave.
