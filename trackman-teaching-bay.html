● Trackman Teaching Bay in Derby & Nottingham | Evolution Golf Academy

Golf lessons near Derby & Nottingham

Our Trackman teaching bay is at Ormonde Fields Golf Club, Codnor, between Derby and Nottingham. Around 20 minutes from Derby city centre, 25 from Nottingham, and minutes from Ripley, Heanor and Ilkeston.

Equipped with industry leading Trackman technology, training aids, mirrors and professional coaching equipment, every lesson is designed to give you the feedback, understanding and support needed to make lasting improvements.

It is where all of our golf lessons in Derby and Nottingham take place, taught one to one by Advanced PGA Professional Will Painter.

Why Does a Private Bay Make a Difference to a Lesson?

Learning a movement requires attention, and attention is the first thing an open range takes away. Golfers self-consciously hitting shots in front of an audience swing faster, rush between balls and stop asking questions. Put the same golfer in a quiet bay with a screen, a mirror and a coach and the questions start coming, which is when a lesson begins to earn its money.

In plain terms, it is a room rather than a bay on a busy range. Nobody is hitting a driver two feet to your left, nobody is waiting for the ball dispenser behind you, and nothing you are told gets lost in the noise. For golfers travelling in from Derby, Nottingham and across Derbyshire, that hour is the part of the week where the work actually gets done.

What Technology Is in the Bay?

Trackman dual radar for ball and club data, high speed video from two angles, full length mirrors for real time feedback on posture and plane, and the training aids an Advanced PGA Professional reaches for when a picture is worth more than an explanation. The same Trackman numbers you see on the Trackman driving range are here, with someone qualified sitting next to them.

The bay is used for every kind of coaching we run, from beginner golf lessons through to low handicap players chasing tighter dispersion, and for the indoor sessions on the monthly coaching programme. Your swing videos are sent to you afterwards, so the lesson does not evaporate on the drive home.

What the Teaching Bay actually gives you

Four things behind every lesson taught in the bay.

Trackman data

Tour proven radar measures club and ball on every shot, so we work from facts rather than from what the strike felt like to either of us.

Video analysis

Every swing recorded alongside its data, from two angles. Your videos are sent home afterwards so you can review them whenever you like.

Performance testing

Skills measured under pressure, so coaching targets what is genuinely costing you shots rather than what is easiest to teach.

Training aids & mirrors

Full length mirrors and the right aid at the right moment, for when seeing the position beats another explanation of it.

Most golfers are not short of tips. They are short of a clear reason why. The bay exists so you leave every lesson knowing exactly what to work on next, and why it will make a difference.

● Top questions from golfers

Golf lessons Derby & Nottingham FAQs

How much is a golf lesson?+
A single one to one lesson is £55 for 60 minutes with our Advanced PGA Professional, with Trackman data and video analysis included. Blocks of lessons and the monthly programme work out cheaper per session and tend to produce better results.
Do I need my own clubs?+
Bring them if you have them, because coaching around the clubs you actually play with is more useful. If you do not have a set, tell us when booking and we will provide clubs at no extra cost.
Am I too old or too much of a beginner for lessons?+
No. We coach complete beginners through to low handicap golfers, and every lesson is built around you rather than a fixed syllabus. Plenty of people take their first lesson having never held a club.
Do I get my swing videos?+
Yes. Your recordings are sent to you after the lesson so you can review them at home, and so you have a genuine before and after to look back on a few months later.
How often should I have lessons?+
Most golfers see the fastest progress on a regular block or the monthly programme rather than one off lessons. A single lesson can fix one clear fault, but lasting change needs a few sessions and some practice in between.
Where do lessons take place?+
In the Trackman teaching bay at Ormonde Fields Golf Club, Codnor, Ripley DE5 9RJ. Some lessons move out to the range or the short game area if that suits what we are working on.
