● Private coaching bay in Derbyshire

What Is a Trackman Teaching Bay?

A great lesson starts with feeling comfortable. That is why our private Trackman Teaching Bay provides a quiet, distraction free environment where you can relax, focus on your game and enjoy a premium coaching experience. Equipped with industry leading Trackman technology, training aids, mirrors and professional coaching equipment, every lesson is designed to give you the feedback, understanding and support needed to make lasting improvements.

In plain terms, it is a room rather than a bay on a busy range. Nobody is hitting a driver two feet to your left, nobody is waiting for the ball dispenser behind you, and nothing you are told gets lost in the noise. For golfers travelling in from Derby, Nottingham and across Derbyshire, that hour is the part of the week where the work actually gets done.

Why Does a Private Bay Make a Difference to a Lesson?

Learning a movement requires attention, and attention is the first thing an open range takes away. Golfers self-consciously hitting shots in front of an audience swing faster, rush between balls and stop asking questions. Put the same golfer in a quiet bay with a screen, a mirror and a coach and the questions start coming, which is when a one to one golf lesson begins to earn its money.

What Technology Is in the Bay?

Trackman dual radar for ball and club data, high speed video from two angles, full length mirrors for real-time feedback on posture and plane, and the training aids an Advanced PGA Professional reaches for when a picture is worth more than an explanation. The same Trackman numbers you see on the Trackman driving range are here, with someone qualified sitting next to them.

The bay is used for every kind of coaching we run, from beginner golf lessons through to low handicap players chasing dispersion, and for the indoor sessions on the monthly coaching programme.

● What is in the bay

What Do You Get in a Trackman Teaching Bay Lesson?

Four things, in every session, whether you are two months into golf or two shots off scratch.

Trackman Data

Club path, face angle, attack angle, strike location, launch, spin and carry, measured on every shot. Your coach uses it to explain exactly why the ball did what it did, rather than guessing from the naked eye.

Trackman Video Analysis

Your swing filmed, drawn on and played back in the lesson, then sent to you afterwards so you have it on your phone. Feel and real are rarely the same thing, and video settles the argument in about four seconds.

Performance and Test Centre

Structured tests that challenge specific areas of your game and expose where you are actually losing shots. Most golfers are surprised. The wedges are usually worse than the driver, whatever the scorecard suggested.

Gamify Your Lesson

Trackman's interactive games turn a drill into a competition with a score attached. Pressure is a skill, and adding a little of it in the bay is the simplest way to make practice look more like golf.

● Why golfers book the bay

Why Take a Golf Lesson in a Private Bay?

The measurable reason is feedback. A Trackman teaching bay golf lesson in Derby gives your coach numbers for every shot and video for the ones that matter, so a change is either working or it is not, and you both find out inside three swings rather than three weeks.

The less measurable reason is that people learn better when they are not being watched. Golf is an unusually public sport to be bad at. A quiet room removes the audience, and the hour turns into a conversation about your game rather than a performance for the bay next door.

● How a lesson runs

How Does a Trackman Golf Lesson Work?

An hour, structured. Nothing about it requires you to know anything about launch monitors beforehand.

1

We talk before you swing

Five minutes on what you play off, what you want, what has been going wrong and what you have already tried. Coaching somebody without that context is guesswork with good equipment.

2

You hit shots and we gather data

Warm up, then hit a set of shots while Trackman records everything and the cameras run. No instruction yet. We need to see your golf swing, not your golf swing plus a suggestion.

3

We look at it together

Data and video on the screen, side by side, with the one or two things actually costing you shots identified in plain English. Long lists of faults are how golfers leave lessons worse than they arrived.

4

We change one thing and test it

A drill, a feel or a training aid, then shots to prove it. Trackman tells us immediately whether the change did what it was supposed to do, so nothing leaves the bay untested.

5

You leave with a plan and the video

What to practise, how to practise it and how you will know it is working. The swing videos are sent to you afterwards. Follow-up work happens on the Trackman range or in the short game area.

● Who books the teaching bay

Who Are Teaching Bay Lessons For?

Golfers Who Have Plateaued

The most common visitor. Someone who got to fifteen or eighteen, stuck there, and has spent two summers hitting balls without moving. Almost always there is a measurable cause hiding in the data, usually strike location or a club path they had no way of seeing on their own.

Complete Beginners

Starting in a quiet bay rather than on a busy range is easier on the nerves and quicker on the learning. New golfers pair the bay with beginner coaching, and the Ladies Academy and Junior Academy both use it for indoor work through the winter.

Better Players Chasing Small Margins

For single figure golfers the gains are in dispersion, spin control and gapping rather than anything dramatic. That work needs measurement, because the differences are too small to see. Half a degree of face angle is invisible to the eye and worth several yards left of the flag.

Anyone Whose Body Is the Problem

A fair number of swing faults are physical rather than technical. If you cannot rotate, you will find another way to move the club, and no amount of instruction fixes that. In those cases the honest answer is a TPI screening and some golf fitness work alongside the lessons.
