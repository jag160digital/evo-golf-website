What is the Trackman Teaching Bay?

The Trackman teaching bay is our premium golf coaching facility for one-to-one lessons and small group coaching. Each session includes live swing data, video analysis and expert instruction from a PGA Professional. Available by booking only.

Real-Time Data

Trackman technology captures every detail of your swing - ball speed, spin rate, launch angle and more. See exactly what's happening and why it's happening.

Video Analysis

High-speed video recording of your swing, compared side-by-side with technique models. Watch your improvement session to session.

Expert Coaching

Personalised instruction from a PGA Professional who uses data to guide your improvement. Customised drills and practice plans for your needs.

Questions About Trackman Coaching
