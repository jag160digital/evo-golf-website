Trackman Teaching Bay.

A great lesson starts with feeling comfortable. That’s why our private Trackman Teaching Bay provides a quiet, distraction free environment where you can relax, focus on your game and enjoy a premium coaching experience. Equipped with industry leading Trackman technology, training aids, mirrors and professional coaching equipment, every lesson is designed to give you the feedback, understanding and support needed to make lasting improvements.

Trackman Data

Trackman is the same radar technology trusted on tour, measuring your club and golf ball on every single shot. Ball speed, launch, spin, carry and club path appear in real time, so you and your golf coach work from facts rather than guesswork.

Trackman Video Analysis

Every swing can be recorded in real time alongside the Trackman data for that shot, allowing you and your coach to clearly identify your swing characteristics. After your lesson, you’ll receive your swing videos to review at home and continue your progress between sessions.

Performance & Test Centre

Rather than simply watching you hit golf balls, we can challenge different areas of your game, identify your strengths and expose where you lose shots under pressure, then build a plan around the areas with the biggest impact on your game.

Gamify Your Lesson

Learning should be enjoyable. Where appropriate, we use Trackman’s interactive games and challenges to bring a fun, competitive element to your coaching, helping you develop skills that transfer onto the golf course.
