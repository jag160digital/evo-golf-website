● Before you book

Common Questions About Golf at Our Academy.

Evolution Golf Academy is a PGA coaching facility at Ormonde Fields Golf Club in Codnor, Ripley - fifteen minutes from Derby and twenty from Nottingham on the A610. We are open 7am to 8pm every day of the year, with free parking on site and no membership required for anything.

The questions below are the ones we get asked most often. If you would rather just talk to somebody, call 07710 582036 or use the contact form and we will come back to you the same day.

Buses run along the A610 corridor between Ripley, Codnor and both cities, but services vary and the academy is easiest to reach by car. Call 07710 582036 if you need to check the nearest stop before travelling.

● Facilities

Trackman Range, Simulator & Facilities.

No. The Trackman driving range is walk-in on a first-come basis, every day from 7am to 8pm. Only private teaching bays need booking.
Trackman is the dual-radar launch monitor used on the PGA Tour. It measures ball speed, club head speed, launch angle, spin rate, carry distance, total distance, shot shape, club path and face angle, displayed live in your bay within about a second of impact.
Simulator bays are booked in advance at £25 an hour, either online or by calling 07710 582036. A group can get a full round done in a fraction of the time it takes on a real course.
Yes. Every bay is covered and floodlit, so the range runs exactly the same in December as it does in June. This is the main reason golfers from both cities practise here through the off season.
Yes. The grass driving range runs through the drier months for golfers who want real turf interaction and honest feedback on their strike. The covered bays are the all-year option.
Yes. The short game area covers chipping, pitching, bunker play and putting, which is where most amateur golfers save strokes fastest.
A training space for the mobility, strength and speed work that supports the golf swing, guided by TPI screening rather than a generic gym programme. See the golf fitness suite page.
● Fitting and screening

Custom Fitting & TPI Screening.

Hayden Berridge PGA. Hayden is a specialist fitter as well as a coach, so a fitting accounts for both your current swing and how it is likely to change as you improve.
No. You receive a full written specification and can take it to any retailer or manufacturer you like. The point is that you end up with the right clubs, not that you buy them here.
A full bag fitting typically runs ninety minutes to two hours. A single club fitting, driver only or irons only, is usually about sixty minutes.
TPI screening is a Titleist Performance Institute movement assessment. It tests how your body actually moves and links each limitation to the swing characteristic it tends to cause, then gives you a prioritised exercise plan.
For most golfers a lesson first is fine. Screening becomes worth doing when a coach keeps asking for a position you physically cannot reach, or if you pick up recurring back, elbow or wrist niggles from playing.
Often more than for a low handicapper. Better players adapt to poor equipment; higher handicappers tend to build compensations around it. Getting lie angle and shaft flex right removes a variable you would otherwise be fighting every round.
