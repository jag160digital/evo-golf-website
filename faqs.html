Golf Lessons in Derby & Nottingham - Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about golf lessons, coaching, the Trackman range and simulator at Evolution Golf Academy near Derby and Nottingham. Can't find what you're looking for? Call us on 07710 582036.

Location & Getting to the Academy

Where is Evolution Golf Academy located?+
Evolution Golf Academy is at Nottingham Road, Codnor, Ripley, Derbyshire, DE5 9RJ. We're on the A610 between Derby and Nottingham. Free parking is available on site.
How far is Evolution Golf Academy from Derby city centre?+
Approximately 15 minutes from Derby city centre via the A610. Straightforward drive with free parking when you arrive.
How far is Evolution Golf Academy from Nottingham?+
Approximately 20 minutes from Nottingham city centre via the A610 through Eastwood and Heanor.
What are the opening hours at Evolution Golf Academy?+
We're open 7am to 8pm every day of the week, 7 days a week. No regular closing day. Call ahead on 07710 582036 if you want to confirm a specific time slot.

Golf Lesson Prices & Booking Near Derby

How much do golf lessons cost in Derby?+
Single 60-minute lessons with a PGA Professional are £55. Our monthly coaching programme is £60/month and gives you structured, ongoing coaching. TPI movement screening is £75 per session.
Do I need experience to have a golf lesson?+
No. We coach complete beginners regularly. Will Painter is particularly experienced with new players and will start exactly where you are - no prior knowledge assumed, no awkward judgement.
Do I need my own golf clubs for a lesson?+
Not for your first session. We have clubs available. If you're new to the game and not sure whether to invest in your own set yet, come for a lesson first - then Hayden can do a custom fitting to make sure you buy the right clubs for your swing.
Which PGA coach will I have for my lesson?+
That depends on what you're working on. Will Painter leads general coaching and works with most players. Scott Hassall handles juniors. Hayden Berridge runs custom fitting sessions. If you're not sure, tell us your goal and we'll match you to the right coach.
What is the monthly golf coaching programme?+
The monthly programme is structured coaching at £60/month. It gives you a planned path to improvement rather than sporadic individual lessons. Most popular with beginners who want a clear route to playing on a course, and with club golfers who want to break a specific handicap barrier.
Do you offer junior golf lessons near Derby?+
Yes. Scott Hassall runs our junior academy for players aged 6-16. Both individual lessons and group sessions are available. See the junior academy page for more detail.
Do you offer ladies golf lessons near Nottingham?+
Yes. Ladies sessions are available for all levels from complete beginners. The environment is relaxed and welcoming. See the ladies coaching page for more detail.

Trackman Range & Facilities

Do I need to book the Trackman range in advance?+
No. The Trackman range is walk-in, no booking needed. All 7 covered bays are available on a first-come basis. Baskets start at £4.
How much does the Trackman driving range cost near Derby?+
Small baskets are £4, large baskets are £11. Private bays for coaching or fitting sessions are £15/hour and need to be booked in advance.
What is Trackman and what does it measure?+
Trackman is a launch monitor system used on the PGA Tour and by the world's best players and coaches. At our range, it measures ball speed, club head speed, launch angle, spin rate, carry distance and shot shape on every shot you hit - in real time, displayed on a screen in front of you.
How do I book a golf simulator session near Derby?+
Simulator bays need to be booked in advance. Call 07710 582036 or use the contact form on this site. Simulator sessions are £25/hour for a private bay.
What golf courses are on the simulator?+
200+ courses including Pebble Beach, St Andrews, Augusta National, TPC Sawgrass, Royal Birkdale and many more. The library is updated regularly.
Is there an outdoor driving range at the academy?+
Yes. We have a grass driving range for outdoor practice when the weather is good, as well as a short game practice area. The 7 covered Trackman bays operate year-round regardless of conditions.

Custom Golf Club Fitting

Who carries out custom golf club fitting at EGA?+
Hayden Berridge PGA. Hayden is a specialist fitter as well as a PGA coach, which means the fitting considers both your current swing and how it's likely to change as you improve.
Do I have to buy clubs from you after a fitting?+
No. You receive a full specification document and can take it to any retailer or manufacturer. We want you to have the right clubs - where you buy them is your choice.
How long does a golf club fitting take?+
A full bag fitting typically takes 90 minutes to 2 hours. Individual club fittings (driver only, irons only) can be done in 60 minutes. Contact us to discuss what you need and we'll confirm the right session length.
