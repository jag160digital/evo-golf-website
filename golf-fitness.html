Golf fitness & TPI screening in Derbyshire

Your golf swing can only do what your body allows. If you cannot rotate, you cannot make the move a coach asks for, no matter how many lessons you take.

The golf fitness suite pairs a TPI movement screen with golf specific strength and mobility work. We find the physical limitation first, then build a swing and a training plan that fit the body you actually have.

It works alongside your golf lessons, and is the reason changes made in the teaching bay tend to stick rather than fade after a fortnight.

What Is TPI Screening and Why Does It Matter?

TPI stands for Titleist Performance Institute, the body that developed the standard movement screen used by tour coaches and physiotherapists worldwide. The screen is a series of physical tests covering rotation, stability, mobility and balance, and it takes around 45 minutes to work through the full set.

The point is not to grade your fitness. It is to find the specific restrictions that make certain swing faults almost inevitable. A golfer who cannot separate their hips from their shoulders will slide rather than turn, and no amount of being told to turn will change that until the restriction is addressed. Screening first means the coaching that follows is aimed at something real.

Do I Need to Be Fit Before I Come?

No, and most golfers who book a screening are not. The screen simply establishes where you are now. Some people leave with three mobility drills to do at home, others with a strength plan, and a few with a referral because what we have found needs a physiotherapist rather than a golf coach.

It is equally useful at the other end. Golfers chasing distance often assume they need to swing harder, when the speed they are missing is locked up in a restricted turn or a lack of ground force. Screening finds that, and the SwingFit programme and TPI screening page cover how we build the training around it.

● Top questions from golfers

Golf fitness & TPI screening FAQs

What is a TPI screening?+
A Titleist Performance Institute movement screen. It is a standard set of physical tests that assesses how your body moves and links that directly to your golf swing, so we can see which faults are technique and which are physical.
How long does it take?+
Around 45 minutes for the full screen, plus time afterwards to talk through your results and agree a plan. Most people are with us for a little over an hour in total.
Do I need to be fit already?+
No. The screen tells us where you are now, and the plan starts from there. Golfers of every age and fitness level book screenings, and a good number have never done structured training in their lives.
Will it help my distance?+
Often yes. Speed usually comes from moving better rather than swinging harder, and the screen finds the restrictions that are capping what you can produce. Distance gains from mobility work tend to arrive without any extra effort on your part.
Is it only for injured golfers?+
No. Many golfers screen to prevent injury and to unlock the movement their swing needs. If you are currently in pain we will tell you honestly whether you need a physiotherapist before you need a golf coach.
Do I need lessons as well?+
They work best together. The screen shapes what your coach can reasonably ask your body to do, which is why we recommend screening before starting a block of golf lessons if you have a physical issue you already know about.
