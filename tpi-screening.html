What Is TPI Screening?

TPI screening is a structured physical assessment developed by the Titleist Performance Institute. It tests how your body moves - rotation, stability, balance and mobility - and then connects each limitation to the swing characteristic it produces. If your body physically cannot make a position, coaching alone will never get you there.

The Movement Screen

A structured series of physical tests covering rotation, stability, mobility and balance. It takes the guesswork out of why your swing does what it does.

Swing Connection

Every limitation found is matched to the swing characteristic it tends to cause - early extension, loss of posture, casting and the rest.

Your Personal Report

You leave with a clear written picture of what your body can and cannot currently do, and what that means for your golf.

Exercise Plan

A prioritised set of exercises targeting the restrictions that will make the biggest difference to your golf swing first.

How TPI Screening Improves Your Golf Lessons.

A coach can spend months chasing a swing fault that is physical rather than technical. Screening first tells your coach which faults are worth working on directly and which need the body addressing before the swing will follow.

  • ✓Stop chasing impossible positions - work with your body, not against it
  • ✓Faster progress - lessons target what is actually fixable now
  • ✓Fewer injuries - compensations are what tend to hurt backs and elbows
  • ✓Feeds the fitness suite - screening results drive your training plan
